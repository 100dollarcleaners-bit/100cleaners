## 1. At-Will Employment / Independent Contractor Status

**Choose the section that applies to your business model and delete the other.**

### For W-2 Employees

Employment with 100% Cleaner is **at-will**. This means either you or the company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice, subject to applicable law. No manager or representative other than the owner in writing may alter this at-will status.

Nothing in this handbook creates a contract of employment or guarantees employment for any specific period.

### For 1099 Independent Contractors

You are engaged as an independent contractor, not an employee. You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, and business expenses unless otherwise agreed in writing. You control the manner and means of completing assigned jobs within 100% Cleaner quality standards and client expectations.

Contractors may accept or decline offered jobs without penalty, provided reasonable notice is given when declining a confirmed assignment.

## 3. Code of Conduct & Professional Standards

Every team member represents 100% Cleaner inside client homes and in the community. Professional conduct is non-negotiable.

### Expected Behavior

- Arrive on time, in uniform, with required supplies
- Greet clients politely; use approved scripts and tone
- Treat every home with respect — no snooping, no gossip, no judgment
- Follow all SOPs and checklists without shortcuts
- Communicate issues (damage, access problems, supply needs) immediately
- Keep phones on silent; personal calls only during approved breaks
- Never smoke, vape, or consume alcohol or drugs on the job

### Prohibited Behavior

- Theft or misuse of client property
- Sharing client information, addresses, or photos without authorization
- Working under the influence of drugs or alcohol
- Verbal abuse, threats, or aggressive behavior toward clients or coworkers
- Accepting cash payments or side jobs from 100% Cleaner clients without company approval
- Bringing unauthorized guests to job sites
- Using client Wi-Fi, streaming services, or food without permission

**Violation of conduct standards may result in immediate termination.**

## 6. Safety & OSHA Compliance

Your safety and client safety come first. California employers must maintain an Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP).

### General Safety Rules

- Read SDS (Safety Data Sheets) for all chemicals you use
- Never mix chemicals (especially bleach and ammonia)
- Use gloves and ventilation as directed
- Report slippery floors, broken equipment, or hazardous conditions immediately
- Use step stools — never stand on counters, tubs, or unstable furniture
- Lift with your legs; ask for help with heavy items
- Keep caddies and cords out of walkways

### Incidents & Injuries

**All injuries must be reported the same day**, even if minor.

1. Ensure the area is safe
2. Administer first aid if trained
3. Notify the office: _______________
4. Complete an Incident Report Form within 24 hours
5. Seek medical attention if needed — do not "tough it out"

See the **Emergency Procedures** document for detailed response protocols.

### Workers' Compensation

W-2 employees: 100% Cleaner carries workers' compensation insurance as required by California law. Report all work-related injuries immediately to access benefits.

1099 contractors: You are responsible for your own insurance coverage.

## 8. Client Property, Privacy & Confidentiality

### Keys & Access Codes

## 12. Disciplinary Process & Termination

### Progressive Discipline (Typical)

1. **Verbal warning** — documented in personnel file
2. **Written warning** — specific improvement plan and timeline
3. **Final written warning** or suspension
4. **Termination**
