## Design Tips

1. **Use one headline + one CTA** — don't clutter the design
2. **Make the phone number the largest text** after the headline on door hangers
3. **QR codes** should link directly to your booking page, not homepage
4. **Include pricing** — LA homeowners want transparency; $150/$300 builds trust
5. **Print 500–1,000 door hangers** for your target neighborhoods and distribute on weekday afternoons (when people are at work and hangers stay on doors)
6. **Track codes** (DOOR25, FLYER25) to measure which materials convert
7. **Compliance:** Check local ordinances on door-to-door solicitation and flyer distribution — some LA neighborhoods restrict commercial door hangers

---

## Distribution Checklist

- [ ] Target neighborhoods within your service radius
- [ ] Focus on areas with single-family homes, condos, and young professionals
- [ ] Pair door hangers with Nextdoor posts in the same ZIP codes
- [ ] Leave hangers on front doors only — never mailboxes (USPS violation)
- [ ] Track bookings with source code "DOOR" or "FLYER"
- [ ] Re-print quarterly with seasonal headline updates
