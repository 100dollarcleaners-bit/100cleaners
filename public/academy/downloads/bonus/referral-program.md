## Program Overview

The **100% Cleaner Referral Program** rewards existing clients who send new customers our way. Referrals are the highest-converting, lowest-cost leads for a local cleaning business — and they build trust before the first phone call.

**Program tagline:** *Love your clean home? Share the shine.*

## How It Works

### For the Referrer (Existing Client)

1. Share your unique referral code or link with friends, family, neighbors, or coworkers in the Los Angeles area.
2. When your referral books their **first paid service** and mentions your name or code at booking, both of you receive a reward.
3. Rewards are applied automatically to your next invoice or sent as a digital gift card within 5 business days of the referral's completed first clean.

### For the New Client (Referral)

1. Book online or call _______________ and mention the referrer's name or code: **_______________**
2. Pay the standard **$25 booking deposit** to secure the appointment.
3. Receive **$25 off** their first service (Regular Clean $150 → **$125** | Deep Clean $300 → **$275**).
4. After their first clean, they receive their own referral code to share.

## Official Program Rules

### Eligibility

- **Referrer:** Must be a current or past 100% Cleaner client in good standing (no outstanding balance, no active dispute).
- **Referral:** Must be a **new client** who has never booked with 100% Cleaner before (verified by phone, email, and service address).
- **Service area:** Referral address must be within the 100% Cleaner service area (Greater Los Angeles — define your ZIP codes: _______________).

### Qualifying Actions

A referral is **qualified** when ALL of the following are true:

1. New client uses the referrer's code or name at **initial booking**
2. New client pays the **$25 deposit**
3. New client completes their **first paid service** (Regular $150, Deep $300, or higher)
4. New client does not cancel or no-show

### Non-Qualifying Referrals

Referrals do **not** qualify if:

- The new client was already in our CRM, quote pipeline, or marketing list
- The referral is a household member at the same service address as the referrer
- The booking is cancelled before service or the client no-shows
- The referrer's account has an outstanding balance at the time of reward issuance
- Fraud is suspected (self-referral, duplicate accounts, fake bookings)

### Reward Limits

- Maximum **$_____________** in referral credits per referrer per calendar year (optional cap)
- Referral credits have **no cash value** and cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash
- Credits expire after **12 months** if unused (optional — disclose clearly)
- One referral reward per new household / service address

### Stacking & Promotions

- Referral discount **cannot be combined** with other first-time promotions unless explicitly stated
- Referrer credits apply to future services only, not the current invoice (unless you choose otherwise)

## Referral Tracking System

### Step 1 — Assign Unique Codes

Generate a simple, memorable code for each client:

| Client Name | Referral Code | Link |
|-------------|---------------|------|
| Jane Smith | JANE25 | 100cleaner.com/book?ref=JANE25 |
| Maria Garcia | MARIA25 | 100cleaner.com/book?ref=MARIA25 |

**Code format suggestion:** FIRSTNAME25 or LASTNAME100

### Step 2 — Track in Your CRM

Create a referral tracking sheet or CRM fields:

| Date | Referrer Name | Referrer Code | New Client Name | New Client Phone | Booking Date | Service Type | Service $ | Deposit $ | Service Completed? | Referrer Rewarded? | New Client Discount Applied? |
|------|---------------|---------------|-----------------|------------------|--------------|--------------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|------------------|-------------------------------|

### Step 3 — Automate Where Possible

- Add a **"How did you hear about us?"** field with "Referral" option on intake form
- Add a **"Referral code"** field at online checkout
- Trigger a thank-you text to the referrer when the referral's first clean is marked complete
- Send the new client their own referral code in the post-service follow-up email

### Step 4 — Monthly Reconciliation

On the 1st of each month, review:

- Pending referrals (booked but not yet completed)
- Rewards owed vs. rewards issued
- Top referrers for personal thank-you outreach

## Referral Program FAQ (for your website)

**How do I refer someone?**
Share your personal code. They enter it when booking online or mention it when they call.

**When do I get my reward?**
Within 5 business days after your referral completes their first paid clean.

**Can I refer multiple people?**
Yes! There's no limit. Some clients earn a free clean every few months.

**Does the referral discount apply to deep cleans?**
Yes — $25 off any first-time service over $100.

**What if my referral forgets to use my code?**
Contact us within 48 hours of their booking and we'll apply it manually if the clean hasn't happened yet.

**Can I use my credit on add-ons?**
Yes — credits apply to any service or add-on.
