# Yelp Masterclass Guide

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Yelp can be a goldmine or a money pit for cleaning companies. This guide covers profile optimization, review strategy, keyword targeting, and when paid Yelp leads actually make sense.

---

## Yelp Reality Check

| Myth | Truth |
|------|-------|
| "Yelp hides good reviews" | Yelp filters suspicious reviews — earn reviews organically and they stick |
| "More reviews = more calls" | **Rating + recent reviews + complete profile** drive calls |
| "Yelp ads always work" | Ads amplify a good profile; they can't fix a bad one |
| "Yelp is dying" | Still top 3 for local home services in most US metros |

**Goal:** Build a profile that converts searchers into booked jobs at positive ROI.

## Phase 1: Profile Setup (Day 1)

### Claim & Verify

1. Search your business at biz.yelp.com
2. Claim listing or create new if missing
3. Verify by phone or email
4. Add all service categories

### Primary Categories (Choose Carefully)

| Priority | Category |
|----------|----------|
| Primary | House Cleaners |
| Secondary | Home Cleaning |
| Additional | Office Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning, Janitorial Services |

### Business Information — Complete 100%

| Field | Best Practice |
|-------|---------------|
| Business name | Exact legal/DBA name — no keyword stuffing |
| Address | Service area city (hide address if home-based per Yelp rules) |
| Phone | Tracking number or main line — answer live |
| Website | Your booking page, not Facebook |
| Hours | Match reality; note "by appointment" if needed |
| Service area | All cities you actually serve (don't overreach) |
| Year established | Builds trust |
| Licenses/insurance | Upload certificates if applicable |

### About Section (750 characters)

**Template:**

> [Your Company Name] provides professional residential and commercial cleaning in [City] and surrounding areas including [City 2], [City 3]. We specialize in recurring maintenance cleans, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out service, and Airbnb turnovers. Every cleaner is background-checked, trained to our SOPs, and insured. Book online at [website] or call [phone] for a same-day quote. Serving [Area] since [Year].

**Keywords to weave naturally:** house cleaning, deep clean, move-out, recurring, [your city name]

## Phase 2: Photos (The Highest-ROI Free Task)

Yelp profiles with **25+ photos** get significantly more engagement.

### Photo Shot List

| Type | Qty | Tips |
|------|-----|------|
| Before/after bathrooms | 5+ | Same angle, good lighting |
| Before/after kitchens | 5+ | Clear counter transformation |
| Team in branded shirts | 3+ | Faces build trust |
| Supplies/equipment | 3+ | Professional caddy, organized |
| Happy client (with permission) | 2+ | Not required but powerful |
| Logo / branded vehicle | 2+ | Recognition |

### Photo Rules

- No stock photos — Yelp and clients spot fakes
- No client-identifying clutter (mail, family photos) without blur
- Upload 2–3 new photos monthly
- Geotag not required but caption with service type

## Phase 3: Keywords & SEO on Yelp

Yelp ranks in Google AND internal search. Optimize for both.

### Target Keywords

Build content around what people search:

- house cleaning [city]
- maid service [city]
- deep cleaning [city]
- move out cleaning [city]
- recurring house cleaner [city]
- Airbnb cleaning [city]

### Where Keywords Go

| Location | How |
|----------|-----|
| Business name | City only if part of legal name — don't spam |
| Categories | Max out relevant categories |
| About section | 2–3 natural keyword mentions |
| Services (if available) | List every service with city |
| Reviews | You can't control text, but happy clients often mention "deep clean" etc. |
| Photo captions | "Deep clean kitchen — [City]" |

### Service Area Strategy

List every city within your **30-minute drive zone** — not the whole state.

## Phase 4: Getting Reviews (The Right Way)

### Yelp Review Policies — Follow Strictly

**Do:**
- Ask happy clients verbally after a great clean
- Send a follow-up text with direct Yelp link
- Put Yelp badge on website and email signature
- Respond to every review (positive and negative)

**Do NOT:**
- Offer discounts for Yelp reviews (prohibited)
- Ask employees to review you
- Buy reviews
- Review gate (only sending happy clients to Yelp)
- Use review solicitation software that violates Yelp TOS

### Review Request Timing

| When | Conversion |
|------|------------|
| Right after compliment | Highest — strike while hot |
| Same day follow-up text | Strong |
| 24-hour email | Good |
| 7 days later | Lower but still worth it |

### Direct Review Link

Find yours: Yelp for Business → Grow your business → Share review request link

**Text template:**

> Hi [Name]! We're so glad you loved today's clean. If you have 60 seconds, a Yelp review helps other [City] families find us: [Yelp Review Link]. Thank you! — [Your Name], [Company]

### Review Response Templates

**5-star:**

> Thank you, [Name]! It was our pleasure cleaning your home in [Neighborhood]. We look forward to your next visit! — [Owner Name], [Company]

**3-star or below:**

> [Name], thank you for the honest feedback. We'd like to make this right — please call me directly at [phone] so we can address your concerns. — [Owner Name]

**Never argue publicly on Yelp.**

## Phase 5: Yelp Ads & Leads — When to Buy

### Yelp Advertising Options

| Product | What It Does | Typical Cost |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Enhanced Profile | Badge, removal of competitor ads on your page | $300–$600/mo |
| Yelp Ads | Appear in search results and competitor pages | $500–$1,500+/mo |
| Cost Per Click (CPC) | Pay when someone clicks | $8–$25/click cleaning |
| Yelp Leads / Connected | Pay per lead (message, call, quote request) | Varies |

### When Yelp Ads Make Sense

**Green lights (consider ads):**
- 4.5+ star rating with 15+ reviews
- Profile 100% complete with 20+ photos
- You answer calls within 2 rings / respond to messages in 1 hour
- You can close 30%+ of leads at your target margin
- You've tracked CPA: Cost Per Acquired customer

**Red lights (fix first, don't advertise):**
- Under 4.0 stars
- Fewer than 10 reviews
- No online booking or slow follow-up
- You don't know your profit per job

### ROI Calculation

```
Monthly Yelp ad spend:     $800
Clicks received:           80
Leads (calls + messages):  20  (25% click-to-lead)
Booked jobs:               6   (30% close rate)
Average job value:         $180
Revenue from Yelp:         $1,080

First-month ROI: ($1,080 - $800) / $800 = 35%

BUT factor in:
- Recurring value (a $150 bi-weekly client = $3,900/year)
- Time to respond
- Callback / quality costs
```

**A $150 bi-weekly client acquired for $800 is a win if they stay 6+ months.**

### Negotiating Yelp

- Yelp reps call constantly — you can negotiate
- Ask for 3-month trial at reduced rate
- Request waived setup fees
- Get CPC cap in writing
- Review contract — many auto-renew

### Yelp vs. Google Ads

| Factor | Yelp | Google |
|--------|------|--------|
| Intent | High — actively searching cleaning | High — broader |
| Cost | Often higher CPC | More control with skill |
| Profile importance | Critical (they see you on Yelp) | Lands on your site |
| Best for | Businesses with strong Yelp presence | Everyone at scale |

**Run both only when each channel is profitable independently.**

## Phase 7: Competitor Intelligence

### Monthly Yelp Audit (15 minutes)

- [ ] Search "house cleaners [your city]" — where do you rank?
- [ ] Click top 3 competitors — what do their profiles have that you don't?
- [ ] Read their negative reviews — what do clients complain about? (That's your marketing angle)
- [ ] Note their pricing mentions in reviews
- [ ] Upload 2 new photos to stay active

### Differentiation Angles Clients Care About

- Background-checked cleaners
- Same cleaner every visit
- Online booking and pay
- Supplies included
- Satisfaction guarantee
- Locally owned

## Phase 8: Handling Negative Yelp Reviews

### Prevention First

- Post-clean text: "Anything we missed? Reply before you review."
- Fix issues within 24 hours
- Document with photos

### If You Get a Bad Review

1. **Breathe** — don't respond for 2 hours
2. **Investigate** — check job notes, photos, cleaner account
3. **Respond professionally** — offer offline resolution
4. **Fix if legitimate** — offer re-clean
5. **Flag only if fake** — competitor, never a client, violates TOS

**Yelp rarely removes reviews.** Your best defense is drowning bad reviews with good ones.
