# Product Shopping List

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

## Color-Coded Microfiber System

| Color | Use Only For |
|-------|-------------|
| Blue | General dusting & living areas |
| Green | Kitchen & food prep surfaces |
| Yellow | Bathrooms & toilets |
| Red | High-risk / first wipe (optional) |
| White | Glass & mirrors |

**Rule:** Never cross-contaminate. One color per zone. Wash separately.

---

## Chemicals

| Product | Use For | Do NOT Use On |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| All-purpose cleaner (neutral pH) | Countertops, most surfaces | Natural stone unsealed |
| Disinfectant (EPA registered) | Bathrooms, high-touch areas | Food prep without rinse |
| Glass cleaner | Mirrors, windows, glass tables | TV screens, coated surfaces |
| Degreaser | Stovetops, hoods, greasy cabinets | Painted walls, unsealed wood |
| Bathroom / tub & tile cleaner | Showers, tubs, tile | Natural stone |
| Stainless steel polish | Appliances, fixtures | Non-stainless metals |
| Floor cleaner (pH neutral) | Hardwood, LVP, tile | Unsealed wood, natural stone |
| Wood cleaner/ polish | Sealed hardwood, furniture | Laminate, unsealed wood |
| Grout cleaner | Tile grout lines | Colored grout (test first) |
| Oven cleaner | Inside oven only | Self-clean cycle while applied |

## Safety Rules

1. Never mix bleach with ammonia or vinegar
2. Label every bottle — date mixed if diluting
3. SDS sheets available for every chemical (download from manufacturer)
4. Wear gloves for bathroom and degreasing work
5. Ventilate when using strong products
6. Client-requested products: confirm in writing, test inconspicuous area first
