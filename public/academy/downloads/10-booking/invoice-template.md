## Invoice Numbering System

Use a consistent format: `INV-YYYY-####`

| Example | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| INV-2025-0001 | First invoice of 2025 |
| INV-2025-0142 | 142nd invoice of 2025 |

Track in spreadsheet: Invoice #, Client, Date, Amount, Paid (Y/N), Payment Method.

---

## Commercial Client Invoice (Net Terms)

For property managers and offices:

```
Payment Terms: Net 15
Due Date: [Invoice Date + 15 days]

PO Number: [if provided]
Property / Unit: [Building name or unit #]
```
