# Floor Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Applies to:** All services — always **vacuum first, mop second** on hard surfaces  
**Time target:** Regular 20–30 min whole home | Deep 30–45 min (includes edges and baseboards)

---

## Purpose

Floors are the last task in every room and the whole home. Clean floors make or break the client's impression. Match method to floor type — one wrong product can damage surfaces permanently.

---

## Supplies

- [ ] Vacuum with hose, crevice tool, and brush attachment
- [ ] Mop system (microfiber flat mop preferred)
- [ ] Bucket OR spray-mop with refillable solution
- [ ] pH-neutral hard floor cleaner
- [ ] Carpet spot cleaner (optional)
- [ ] Dry microfiber pad for final buff
- [ ] "Wet floor" caution if client has pets/elderly (optional sign)

---

## Universal Rule: Vacuum → Mop

**Hard surfaces (hardwood, tile, LVP, laminate):**

1. [ ] Vacuum entire floor including edges and under furniture edges
2. [ ] Mop with appropriate cleaner — damp, not soaking
3. [ ] Dry buff high-traffic areas if needed

**Carpet:**

1. [ ] Vacuum only — no mopping
2. [ ] Use crevice tool at carpet-to-baseboard edges
3. [ ] Spot treat stains only if in scope and product-safe

**Never mop before vacuuming** — you'll push debris and scratch floors.

---

## Whole-Home Floor Order

- [ ] Complete all dry work (dusting, counters, beds) before any mopping
- [ ] Vacuum all carpeted areas first
- [ ] Vacuum all hard floors (pick up debris mop would spread)
- [ ] Mop hard floors **back to front**, **far corner toward exit**
- [ ] Start at back of house; finish at entry door
- [ ] Do not walk back across wet floors

---

## Floor Type Guide

### Hardwood (Sealed)

- [ ] Vacuum with hard floor setting or brush off
- [ ] Mop with **pH-neutral hardwood cleaner** — damp microfiber only
- [ ] Wring mop until barely damp — water is hardwood's enemy
- [ ] Mop with the grain when possible
- [ ] Dry buff with clean microfiber if streaks appear

**Do NOT:** Soak floors, use steam mop on unsealed wood, use vinegar on waxed floors, use abrasive pads.

---

### Tile & Grout

- [ ] Vacuum or sweep to remove grit (grit scratches tile)
- [ ] Mop with all-purpose or tile cleaner
- [ ] Change mop water when gray
- [ ] Deep clean: hand-scrub grout lines in kitchen and entry

**Do NOT:** Use bleach on colored grout without testing. Avoid acid cleaners on natural stone tile.

---

### LVP / Vinyl Plank / Laminate

- [ ] Vacuum with hard floor setting — no beater bar on loose planks
- [ ] Mop **lightly damp** — laminate swells with excess water
- [ ] Use manufacturer-approved or pH-neutral cleaner
- [ ] Never let water sit in seams or edges

**Do NOT:** Steam mop on laminate (unless manufacturer explicitly allows). No wax or polish.

---

### Carpet

- [ ] Slow, overlapping vacuum passes — 2 directions in high traffic
- [ ] Crevice tool: edges along baseboards, stairs, and under furniture lips
- [ ] Move lightweight furniture (dining chairs, ottomans) if in scope
- [ ] Spot treat with carpet cleaner — blot, never rub
- [ ] Pet hair: vacuum slow; use rubber glove or pet tool if heavy

**Do NOT:** Over-wet carpet. No bleach. Don't guarantee stain removal.

---

### Area Rugs on Hard Floor

- [ ] Roll edge or lift corner to vacuum under rug edge
- [ ] Vacuum rug top, then flip if client allows (deep clean)
- [ ] Vacuum hard floor beneath before replacing rug

---

## Edge & Detail Work

Clients notice edges. Every deep clean and move-out includes:

- [ ] Crevice tool along all baseboards on carpet
- [ ] Vacuum under couch and bed **lip** (don't move heavy furniture alone)
- [ ] Kitchen: under toe-kick if vacuum hose reaches
- [ ] Stairs: vacuum each tread, crevice at wall edge, mop hard stair treads if applicable
- [ ] Entry mat — lift and clean under

---

## Mop Technique

- [ ] Fill bucket with clean solution per product dilution
- [ ] Dip and **wring thoroughly** — floors should dry in 2–5 minutes
- [ ] Mop in figure-8 or S-pattern, overlapping passes
- [ ] Change water when dirty or every 2–3 rooms
- [ ] Rinse mop head between rooms on deep cleans
- [ ] Exit room and let dry before closing door

---

## Quality Standards

Floors pass when:

- [ ] No visible crumbs, hair, or debris on any surface
- [ ] Hard floors are not sticky or cloudy with residue
- [ ] Carpet shows vacuum lines in main traffic areas
- [ ] Edges along baseboards are clean (deep/move-out)
- [ ] No wet footprints left behind when you lock up

---

## Do NOT (Floors)

- ❌ Use one mop water for the entire house on deep cleans
- ❌ Mop yourself into a room with no exit
- ❌ Use bleach mop solution on hardwood or LVP
- ❌ Drag vacuum cord across wet floors
- ❌ Leave kitchen mat down and mop around it — lift and clean under
- ❌ Rush carpet vacuum — one fast pass is not a clean
- ❌ Use steam on floors without confirming type with client/notes
