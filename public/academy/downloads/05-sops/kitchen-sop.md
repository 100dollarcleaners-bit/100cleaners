# Kitchen Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Applies to:** Regular ($150), Deep ($300), Move-Out, and Airbnb turnovers  
**Time target:** Regular 25–30 min | Deep 45–60 min | Move-out 90–120 min

---

## Purpose

The kitchen is the highest-risk area for cross-contamination and the most scrutinized room by clients. Follow color-coding and work **clean to dirty, top to bottom**.

---

## Supplies

- [ ] All-purpose cleaner (pH-neutral)
- [ ] Degreaser (deep/move-out)
- [ ] Glass cleaner
- [ ] Microfiber cloths — **RED for kitchen only**
- [ ] Scrub pad (non-scratch)
- [ ] Stainless steel cleaner (if applicable)
- [ ] Disinfectant for sink and food-prep surfaces
- [ ] Trash bags
- [ ] Gloves

**Separate from bathroom supplies at all times.**

---

## Cross-Contamination Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Red cloths = kitchen only | Prevents bathroom bacteria on food surfaces |
| Clean sink last (before final wipe) | Sink collects runoff from dirty tasks |
| Never set toilet brush or bath cloths on counters | Obvious but happens when rushing |
| Use separate trash bag for raw meat packaging if found | Dispose immediately, wash hands |
| Disinfect cutting board areas if visible | Client food prep surface |
| Rinse cloths between zones: counters → appliances → sink | Don't spread grease backward |

---

## Kitchen Order of Operations

Work **top to bottom**, **dry before wet**, **back of counters forward**.

### Phase 1 — Upper Area (Dry)
- [ ] Remove items blocking surfaces (don't reorganize — group and replace)
- [ ] Dust/wipe top of fridge and cabinet tops (if reachable and in scope)
- [ ] Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
- [ ] Clean microwave interior, then exterior
- [ ] Wipe small appliances (toaster, coffee maker exterior)
- [ ] Wipe backsplash

### Phase 2 — Countertops & Stovetop
- [ ] Clear crumbs and debris into trash (not sink)
- [ ] Apply degreaser to stovetop if deep clean — dwell 3–5 min
- [ ] Wipe counters back-to-front with clean microfiber
- [ ] Scrub stovetop, burners/grates (remove only if you know how to reinstall)
- [ ] Wipe range hood exterior and filter cover
- [ ] Clean control panels with slightly damp cloth only — no soaking

### Phase 3 — Sink & Faucet (Disinfect Last)
- [ ] Remove dishes **only if in scope** (dishes add-on +$25)
- [ ] Rinse sink basin
- [ ] Apply disinfectant; scrub basin, faucet, handles, and drain area
- [ ] Polish faucet dry for shine
- [ ] Wipe surrounding counter one final time

### Phase 4 — Lower Area & Floor
- [ ] Wipe lower cabinet fronts and drawer pulls
- [ ] Wipe dishwasher exterior and handle
- [ ] Clean fridge exterior — handles, front, sides if accessible
- [ ] Sweep kitchen floor
- [ ] Mop last (see Floor SOP)

## Stovetop Technique

- [ ] Remove loose grates/knobs only if trained
- [ ] Scrape burnt residue with plastic scraper (not metal on glass tops)
- [ ] Degrease, dwell, scrub with non-scratch pad
- [ ] Wipe dry — streak-free on glass cooktops
- [ ] Replace grates aligned properly

**Glass cooktop:** Use cooktop-specific cleaner only. No abrasive powder.

---

## Stainless Steel

- [ ] Wipe **with the grain**
- [ ] Use stainless cleaner or diluted dish soap — never pure bleach
- [ ] Dry immediately to prevent water spots
- [ ] Handles and fridge front are review points — don't skip

---

## Quality Standards

Kitchen passes when:

- [ ] Counters are clear, crumb-free, and not greasy to the touch
- [ ] Sink shines and smells neutral
- [ ] Stovetop has no visible splatter or burnt-on spots (deep/move-out)
- [ ] Appliance exteriors are fingerprint-free
- [ ] Floor is swept and mopped — check under kitchen mat
- [ ] No cross-contamination cloths left on food surfaces

---

## Do NOT (Kitchen)

- ❌ Use the same cloth on floor then counter
- ❌ Mix bleach with any other product
- ❌ Soak control panels or electrical outlets
- ❌ Use steel wool on any kitchen surface
- ❌ Pour grease down client drains
- ❌ Open client food containers or eat client food
- ❌ Move knife blocks or heavy appliances alone
- ❌ Use bathroom disinfectant on food prep surfaces without rinsing (check product label)
