# Bathroom Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Applies to:** All residential and STR services  
**Time target:** Regular 20–25 min | Deep 35–45 min | Move-out 45–60 min per full bath

---

## Purpose

Bathrooms require true **disinfection**, not just wiping. Follow the toilet-to-door order to avoid re-contaminating clean surfaces. Half baths follow the same order minus shower/tub.

---

## Supplies

- [ ] Bathroom disinfectant (EPA-registered preferred)
- [ ] Toilet bowl cleaner + dedicated toilet brush
- [ ] Calcium/lime remover (deep clean — showers, faucets)
- [ ] Glass cleaner
- [ ] Microfiber cloths — **BLUE for bathroom only**
- [ ] Scrub pad or non-scratch sponge
- [ ] Grout brush (deep/move-out)
- [ ] Gloves (required)
- [ ] Trash bags
- [ ] Shower squeegee (optional — speeds glass drying)

**Never use kitchen cloths in the bathroom.**

---

## Disinfecting Protocol

1. **Apply disinfectant** to target surface
2. **Dwell** per product label (typically 3–10 minutes — do not wipe immediately)
3. **Scrub** if needed (toilet bowl, tub, grout)
4. **Rinse** only if product requires it
5. **Dry** mirrors and chrome for streak-free finish

**High-touch disinfect points every visit:**
- Toilet flush handle
- Faucet handles
- Light switches
- Door handles (both sides if in scope)

---

## Bathroom Order: Toilet → Door

Work from the **dirtiest fixture toward the exit**. Finish at the door so you don't walk back through wet floors.

| Step | Area | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Toilet | Bowl, seat, lid, tank, base, behind toilet |
| 2 | Shower / tub | Walls, glass, fixtures, drain |
| 3 | Sink & counter | Basin, faucet, counter, vanity front |
| 4 | Mirror & fixtures | Glass, towel bars, TP holder |
| 5 | Floor | Sweep, then mop — work toward door |
| 6 | Exit | Wipe door handle last; leave door open to dry |

---

## Step-by-Step Checklist

### Toilet
- [ ] Apply bowl cleaner under rim; let dwell
- [ ] Spray disinfectant on exterior: seat (top and bottom), lid, tank, base
- [ ] Scrub bowl with toilet brush — under rim and waterline
- [ ] Wipe exterior top to bottom with bathroom cloth
- [ ] Clean flush handle and hinges
- [ ] Wipe floor around base and behind toilet (move carefully — don't break wax ring)

### Shower / Tub
- [ ] Remove hair from drain
- [ ] Apply appropriate product for soap scum (dwell 5+ min on deep cleans)
- [ ] Scrub walls, tub floor, and fixtures
- [ ] Squeegee glass doors or wipe shower curtain bottom
- [ ] Polish faucet and showerhead (exterior)
- [ ] Wipe shampoo bottles **only if in scope** — don't rearrange client products without permission

### Sink & Counter
- [ ] Spray disinfectant on basin and counter
- [ ] Scrub sink basin and drain area
- [ ] Clean faucet and handles
- [ ] Wipe counter back-to-front
- [ ] Wipe vanity front and sides
- [ ] Clean inside vanity **only if empty and in scope** (move-out)

### Mirror & Chrome
- [ ] Clean mirror with glass cleaner — top to bottom, dry with clean cloth
- [ ] Polish chrome fixtures dry
- [ ] Wipe towel bars, hooks, and toilet paper holder

### Floor
- [ ] Sweep corners and behind toilet
- [ ] Mop from far corner toward door
- [ ] Do not mop yourself into a corner — plan exit path first

---

## Half Bath (Powder Room)

Skip shower/tub steps. Time target: 10–15 min.

- [ ] Toilet (full protocol)
- [ ] Sink, mirror, counter
- [ ] Empty trash
- [ ] Floor sweep and mop

---

## Deep Clean / Move-Out Additions

- [ ] Grout scrub on discolored lines
- [ ] Descale faucet and showerhead (exterior; soak cloth with CLR if approved)
- [ ] Remove soap scum from glass (razor scraper on glass only if trained)
- [ ] Wipe inside vanity drawers and cabinets (empty only)
- [ ] Clean exhaust fan cover (exterior wipe)
- [ ] Detail caulk lines — scrub mildew stains (report if caulk needs replacement)

---

## Quality Standards

Bathroom passes when:

- [ ] Toilet is white, odor-free, and exterior is dry/clean
- [ ] No hair in drain or on tub ledge
- [ ] Mirror is streak-free
- [ ] Chrome shines without water spots
- [ ] Floor is dry enough to walk safely
- [ ] Smells fresh — not overpowering chemical

---

## Do NOT (Bathroom)

- ❌ Use the toilet brush on any other surface
- ❌ Wipe the sink then go back to the toilet with the same cloth
- ❌ Mix bleach and ammonia (deadly fumes)
- ❌ Use abrasive powder on fiberglass tubs or acrylic showers
- ❌ Stand on tub edge or toilet tank — use step stool
- ❌ Flush client items (jewelry, contacts) — check before scrubbing
- ❌ Ignore mold on caulk — photo, report, don't promise removal
- ❌ Leave wet hair on shower walls

---

## STR / Airbnb Notes

- [ ] Replace all towels with fresh folded set per host standard
- [ ] Minimum 2 rolls toilet paper visible
- [ ] Toilet lid **down** for staging photos
- [ ] Amenity bottles wiped and aligned facing forward
