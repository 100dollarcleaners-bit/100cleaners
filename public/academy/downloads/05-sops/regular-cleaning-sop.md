# Regular / Maintenance Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Service type:** Standard maintenance clean  
**Target home:** Up to 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms  
**Price reference:** $150  
**Time target:** 2–3 hours (solo cleaner) | 1.5–2 hours (2-person team)

---

## Purpose

Maintain a already-clean home between deep cleans. Focus on surfaces, floors, bathrooms, and kitchen reset — not heavy buildup or inside appliances.

---

## Supplies Checklist

Bring before entering the home:

- [ ] All-purpose cleaner (pH-neutral preferred)
- [ ] Glass cleaner
- [ ] Bathroom disinfectant
- [ ] Microfiber cloths (8–12, color-coded)
- [ ] Scrub pads (non-scratch)
- [ ] Vacuum with hose attachment
- [ ] Mop + bucket OR flat mop system
- [ ] Duster / extendable duster
- [ ] Trash bags
- [ ] Gloves
- [ ] Shoe covers or indoor-only shoes
- [ ] Caddy or cleaning tote

**Optional (if in scope):** Furniture polish, stainless steel cleaner, pet hair roller

---

## Pre-Job Setup (5 minutes)

- [ ] Review job notes, access instructions, and client preferences
- [ ] Confirm pets are secured and fragile items noted
- [ ] Take "before" photos if required by your company policy
- [ ] Set up caddy; load vacuum and mop last (exit order)
- [ ] Open windows or turn on ventilation if using strong products

---

## Room Order (Top to Bottom, Back to Front)

Work **dry before wet**, **top before bottom**, **back of house toward exit**.

| Order | Room / Area | Time Target |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Master bedroom | 20–25 min |
| 2 | Additional bedrooms | 15 min each |
| 3 | Hallways & stairs | 10 min |
| 4 | Living / dining / office | 20–25 min |
| 5 | Bathrooms (see Bathroom SOP) | 20–25 min each |
| 6 | Kitchen (see Kitchen SOP) | 25–30 min |
| 7 | Floors — whole home (see Floor SOP) | 20–30 min |
| 8 | Final walk-through | 5 min |

---

## Standard Room Procedure (Bedrooms & Living Areas)

- [ ] Strip and remake beds **only if in scope** (most regular cleans: make beds only)
- [ ] Dust all reachable surfaces top to bottom (shelves, nightstands, TV stands, décor)
- [ ] Wipe light switches, door handles, and remotes
- [ ] Straighten pillows, fold throws, align furniture
- [ ] Empty trash; replace liner
- [ ] Spot-clean mirrors and glass surfaces
- [ ] Vacuum rugs and carpet edges; note hard floors for final mop pass

---

## Kitchen — Regular Scope (Summary)

- [ ] Clear and wipe countertops
- [ ] Wipe exterior of appliances (fridge, microwave, dishwasher, stove top)
- [ ] Clean sink and faucet (disinfect)
- [ ] Wipe cabinet fronts (handles and touch points)
- [ ] Wipe dining table and chairs if in kitchen area
- [ ] **Do NOT** clean inside oven, fridge, or cabinets unless add-on purchased

*Full procedure: see Kitchen SOP*

---

## Bathroom — Regular Scope (Summary)

- [ ] Disinfect toilet (inside bowl, seat, base, handle)
- [ ] Scrub sink, faucet, and counter
- [ ] Clean mirror (streak-free)
- [ ] Scrub shower/tub surfaces (soap scum as needed)
- [ ] Wipe exterior of vanity and fixtures
- [ ] Empty trash; replace liner
- [ ] Sweep and mop floor last in room

*Full procedure: see Bathroom SOP*

---

## Quality Standards

A regular clean passes when:

- [ ] No visible dust on surfaces at eye level
- [ ] Bathrooms smell fresh; fixtures shine, no hair in drains
- [ ] Kitchen counters and sink are clear and wiped
- [ ] Floors are vacuumed/mopped with no debris or sticky spots
- [ ] Trash emptied in all rooms
- [ ] Home looks "guest ready" — tidy, not sterile

---

## Do NOT (Regular Clean)

- ❌ Move or reorganize client belongings without permission
- ❌ Use bleach on colored surfaces, wood, or marble
- ❌ Mix cleaning chemicals (never mix bleach + ammonia)
- ❌ Clean inside appliances unless add-on is on the work order
- ❌ Rush floors — wet floors last, always
- ❌ Leave product residue on stainless steel or glass
- ❌ Skip gloves when handling trash or bathroom work

---

## Final Walk-Through

- [ ] Reset thermostat, lights, and locks per client instructions
- [ ] Return keys / lock door / mark job complete in app
- [ ] Take "after" photos if required
- [ ] Note any damage, pests, or maintenance issues for the client
