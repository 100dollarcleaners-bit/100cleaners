# Airbnb / Short-Term Rental Turnover SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Service type:** Guest checkout to check-in ready  
**Target property:** Furnished STR — 1–3 bedrooms typical  
**Price reference:** $125–$200  
**Time target:** 2–3 hours (hard deadline — next guest may arrive same day)

---

## Purpose

Reset the property to **photo-ready, 5-star review** condition between guests. Speed matters, but never skip the items guests photograph and review: beds, bathroom, kitchen, and "does it smell clean?"

---

## Supplies Checklist

- [ ] All-purpose cleaner, glass cleaner, bathroom disinfectant
- [ ] Microfiber cloths (color-coded: kitchen / bath / general)
- [ ] Vacuum + hose attachment
- [ ] Mop system
- [ ] Fresh trash bags
- [ ] Gloves
- [ ] Linen bag (separate dirty from clean)
- [ ] Host-provided restock kit OR your backup stock (confirm with owner)
- [ ] Phone for staging photos
- [ ] Checklist from host (Wi-Fi card, lock code, amenity list)

**Host should supply:** Clean linen sets, towel sets, toiletries, coffee/tea, paper goods

---

## Turnover Timeline (2–3 Hour Target)

| Block | Task | Time |
|-------|------|------|
| 0:00–0:15 | Walk-through, air out, strip beds, start laundry | 15 min |
| 0:15–0:45 | Bathrooms (both if applicable) | 30 min |
| 0:45–1:15 | Kitchen reset | 30 min |
| 1:15–1:45 | Bedrooms — dust, make beds, stage | 30 min |
| 1:45–2:05 | Living / dining — dust, straighten, restock | 20 min |
| 2:05–2:30 | Floors — vacuum all, mop hard surfaces | 25 min |
| 2:30–2:50 | Final staging, restock, photos | 20 min |
| 2:50–3:00 | Lock up, mark complete, message host | 10 min |

---

## Step 1 — Arrival & Walk-Through

- [ ] Enter and open windows/doors for 5 minutes (weather permitting)
- [ ] Check for damage, missing items, or guest issues — photo and report immediately
- [ ] Note checkout time vs your arrival — document late checkout for host
- [ ] Strip all beds; place dirty linens in bag — **never on floor**
- [ ] Start washer if in-unit laundry (see Laundry SOP)
- [ ] Empty all trash; replace liners in every bin

---

## Step 2 — Bathrooms

- [ ] Disinfect toilet (bowl, seat, base, handle)
- [ ] Clean sink, counter, mirror (streak-free)
- [ ] Scrub shower/tub — remove hair from drain
- [ ] Wipe fixtures and replace towels with **fresh folded set** per host standard
- [ ] Restock: toilet paper (minimum 2 rolls visible), soap, shampoo if provided
- [ ] Sweep and mop floor
- [ ] Close shower curtain neatly or squeegee glass doors

*Full procedure: see Bathroom SOP*

---

## Step 3 — Kitchen

- [ ] Run dishwasher if full of guest dishes (or hand-wash if no dishwasher)
- [ ] Wipe all counters, stovetop, and appliance exteriors
- [ ] Clean sink and faucet
- [ ] Wipe dining table and chairs
- [ ] Check inside microwave — wipe if splattered
- [ ] Restock: coffee, tea, sugar, dish soap, sponge (per host list)
- [ ] Take trash out — guests notice overflowing bins in reviews

*Full procedure: see Kitchen SOP*

---

## Step 4 — Bedrooms

- [ ] Dust surfaces (nightstands, dressers, headboards)
- [ ] Make beds hotel-style:
  - [ ] Fresh fitted sheet — tight hospital corners
  - [ ] Flat sheet tucked evenly
  - [ ] Duvet/comforter centered and smooth
  - [ ] Pillows fluffed, shams aligned
- [ ] Check under beds and closets for left items
- [ ] Empty trash
- [ ] Align curtains/blinds evenly

---

## Step 5 — Living / Common Areas

- [ ] Fluff and align all couch cushions and pillows
- [ ] Dust coffee table, TV stand, shelves
- [ ] Wipe remotes and light switches
- [ ] Straighten décor to match listing photos
- [ ] Check TV works (quick power-on if host requests)
- [ ] Reset thermostat per host instructions

---

## Restock Checklist (Confirm with Host)

Standard STR restock — adjust per property:

- [ ] Toilet paper (2+ rolls per bath)
- [ ] Paper towels
- [ ] Hand soap (kitchen + bath)
- [ ] Shampoo/conditioner/body wash (if provided)
- [ ] Coffee pods / tea
- [ ] Trash bags
- [ ] Spare dish sponge
- [ ] Welcome card / Wi-Fi info visible

---

## Photo Staging Protocol

Hosts need photos when something is wrong OR for quality assurance. Take these every turnover:

- [ ] Made bed — straight-on, well-lit
- [ ] Bathroom — toilet lid down, towels folded, mirror clean
- [ ] Kitchen — clear counters, sink empty
- [ ] Living area — staged like listing photo
- [ ] Any damage or maintenance issue (close-up + wide shot)

Upload to host portal within 30 minutes of completion.

---

## Quality Standards (5-Star Test)

- [ ] Property smells clean (not chemical-heavy — light ventilation)
- [ ] Beds look like a hotel — no wrinkles, pillows uniform
- [ ] Bathroom is hair-free and shiny
- [ ] Kitchen has no dishes in sink
- [ ] Floors are vacuumed — check entry mat and under dining table
- [ ] Matches listing photos at a glance

---

## Do NOT (Airbnb Turnover)

- ❌ Reuse guest linens — always fresh from host supply or your laundry
- ❌ Leave personal items from previous guest (check drawers and under beds)
- ❌ Ignore a broken item — always report same day
- ❌ Rearrange furniture away from listing layout
- ❌ Use strongly scented products (guests may be sensitive)
- ❌ Skip the 2-hour deadline without notifying host immediately
- ❌ Eat food or use amenities left by guests

---

## Emergency Escalation

Report to host **before leaving** if you find:

- Broken appliances, leaks, or active water damage
- Biohazards, pests, or excessive mess beyond normal turnover
- Missing linens or keys
- Guest still in property at your arrival time
