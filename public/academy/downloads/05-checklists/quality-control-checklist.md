## Arrival & Setup Standards

- [ ] Cleaner arrived on time (within agreed window)
- [ ] Uniform presentable — branded or all-black, clean, professional
- [ ] Shoe covers or indoor shoes used per client preference
- [ ] Supplies and equipment organized and appropriate for job type
- [ ] Client access instructions followed correctly

## Kitchen

- [ ] Countertops wiped and free of crumbs, stains, and water spots
- [ ] Stovetop and backsplash clean (grease and splatter removed)
- [ ] Sink scrubbed, polished, and free of food debris
- [ ] Exterior of appliances wiped (fridge, microwave, dishwasher, oven)
- [ ] Cabinet fronts wiped (fingerprints and smudges removed)
- [ ] Floor swept/vacuumed and mopped — edges and corners addressed

## Bathrooms

- [ ] Toilet cleaned inside and out (seat, lid, base, behind)
- [ ] Shower/tub scrubbed — soap scum and mildew addressed
- [ ] Sink and faucet polished — no toothpaste or water spots
- [ ] Mirror streak-free
- [ ] Counter and vanity wiped; items straightened (not rearranged without permission)
- [ ] Floor clean; baseboards wiped where accessible

## Bedrooms & Living Areas

- [ ] Beds made neatly (if in scope) — pillows aligned, linens smooth
- [ ] Surfaces dusted — furniture, shelves, décor, window sills
- [ ] Light switches and door handles wiped
- [ ] Trash emptied; liners replaced
- [ ] Floors vacuumed/mopped; visible debris removed under furniture edges

## Floors & Finishing Touches

- [ ] All rooms vacuumed or mopped per surface type
- [ ] High-traffic areas and entryways addressed
- [ ] Baseboards wiped in main living areas (deep/move-out standard)
- [ ] No wet floors left without signage if client is home
- [ ] Supplies packed; nothing left behind

## Documentation & Client Experience

- [ ] Before/after photos uploaded per company SOP
- [ ] Job notes completed in CRM or scheduling app
- [ ] No damage reported — or damage documented and communicated immediately
- [ ] Client feedback solicited (if present) or follow-up message sent
- [ ] Any issues flagged for re-clean or coaching within 24 hours
