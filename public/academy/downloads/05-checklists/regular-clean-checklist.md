## Kitchen

- [ ] Clear and wipe all countertops
- [ ] Clean stovetop and control knobs
- [ ] Wipe backsplash (spot clean grease splatter)
- [ ] Scrub and polish sink and faucet
- [ ] Wipe exterior of microwave, refrigerator, and dishwasher
- [ ] Wipe cabinet fronts (fingerprints and smudges)
- [ ] Empty trash; replace liner
- [ ] Sweep and mop floor — including under movable items at edges
- [ ] Wipe table and chairs (if in kitchen)

## Bathrooms (Each)

- [ ] Scrub toilet inside, seat, lid, base, and behind
- [ ] Clean shower/tub — soap scum and surface grime
- [ ] Scrub sink and polish faucet/fixtures
- [ ] Clean mirror — streak-free
- [ ] Wipe counter, vanity, and cabinet fronts
- [ ] Empty trash; replace liner
- [ ] Sweep and mop floor
- [ ] Wipe light switches and door handles
- [ ] Straighten towels and bath mats

## Bedrooms (Each)

- [ ] Make bed(s) — linens smooth, pillows aligned
- [ ] Dust nightstands, dressers, and headboards
- [ ] Dust lamps, picture frames, and décor
- [ ] Wipe mirrors (if present)
- [ ] Empty trash; replace liner
- [ ] Vacuum carpet or sweep/mop hard floors
- [ ] Light dust window sills and ledges

## Living & Dining Areas

- [ ] Dust all accessible surfaces — tables, shelves, TV stand, mantel
- [ ] Dust lamps, picture frames, and decorative items
- [ ] Wipe coffee table and dining table
- [ ] Straighten cushions and throw pillows
- [ ] Empty trash; replace liner
- [ ] Vacuum upholstery (light pass) if needed
- [ ] Vacuum carpet or sweep/mop hard floors
- [ ] Dust window sills and ledges

## Floors — Whole Home

- [ ] Vacuum all carpeted areas — including edges and under furniture fronts
- [ ] Sweep and mop all hard floors
- [ ] Entryway and high-traffic paths addressed
- [ ] Spot-clean visible stains on carpet or hard surfaces

## Finishing (All Rooms)

- [ ] Wipe interior door handles and light switches in main areas
- [ ] Quick final walkthrough — nothing missed in scope
- [ ] Supplies packed; home secured per Leaving Checklist
