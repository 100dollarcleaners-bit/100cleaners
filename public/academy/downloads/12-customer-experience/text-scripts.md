# Text Message Scripts

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Texting is the highest-response channel for cleaning companies. Keep messages under 320 characters when possible. Always identify your company. Get SMS consent at booking.

---

## Booking & Confirmation

### 1. New Lead — Quote Follow-Up

```
Hi [Name]! This is [Your Name] from [Company]. Thanks for your interest in
cleaning service for your [X] bed / [X] bath in [City]. Your quote: $[Price]
for [service type]. Book here: [link] or reply to schedule. — [Company]
```

### 2. Booking Confirmation

```
✓ You're booked! [Company]
📅 [Day, Date] at [Time]
📍 [Address]
Service: [Regular/Deep] Clean — $[Price]
Reply CHANGE to reschedule (48hr notice) or QUESTIONS anytime.
```

### 3. Deposit Received

```
Thanks [Name]! We received your $[Amount] deposit. Your [service] is
confirmed for [Date] at [Time]. Balance of $[Amount] due after service.
— [Company]
```

## Reminders

### 4. 48-Hour Reminder

```
Hi [Name]! Reminder: [Company] is scheduled [Day] at [Time] at [Address].
Please ensure we have access (key/code/gate). Reply YES to confirm or
RESCHEDULE if needed. Thanks!
```

### 5. Day-Before Reminder

```
Tomorrow! [Company] arrives between [Time window] for your [service type].
Any special instructions? Reply here. Need to reschedule? 48hr notice
applies — see our policy: [link]
```

### 6. On the Way (Cleaner En Route)

```
Hi [Name]! [Cleaner Name] from [Company] is on the way and should arrive
by [Time]. See you soon!
```

### 7. Arrived — Access Issue

```
Hi [Name], our team is at [Address] but we can't get in ([wrong code / no
answer]). Please reply ASAP or call [phone]. We'll wait 15 min per our policy.
```

## Texting Rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Send 8 AM – 8 PM local | Text before 8 AM or after 9 PM |
| Identify company in first text | Assume they saved your number |
| Keep under 2 texts per interaction | Send 5-message walls |
| Get opt-in at booking | Cold-text purchased lists |
| Log in CRM | Lose conversation history |
