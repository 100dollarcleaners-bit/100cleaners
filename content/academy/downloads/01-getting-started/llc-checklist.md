# LLC Formation Checklist

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

> Consult a CPA or attorney in your state. Requirements vary by location.

## Before You File

- [ ] Choose your business name (check availability with Secretary of State)
- [ ] Search USPTO trademark database for conflicts
- [ ] Reserve domain name and social handles
- [ ] Decide: single-member LLC vs multi-member
- [ ] Choose registered agent (yourself or a service)

## Filing Steps

- [ ] File Articles of Organization with your state
- [ ] Pay state filing fee (typically $50–$500)
- [ ] Receive confirmation / certificate of formation
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS (free at irs.gov — do NOT pay third parties)
- [ ] Open dedicated business bank account
- [ ] Apply for business credit card (optional, separate from personal)

## After Formation

- [ ] Create Operating Agreement (even for single-member LLCs)
- [ ] Obtain general liability insurance ($1M minimum recommended)
- [ ] Obtain bonding if required in your market
- [ ] Apply for local business license / home occupation permit if needed
- [ ] Register for state sales tax if applicable
- [ ] Set up business phone line (Google Voice, OpenPhone, or carrier)
- [ ] Create business email (e.g., hello@yourcompany.com)
- [ ] Update Google Business Profile with legal business name

## Financial Setup

- [ ] Connect business bank to accounting software (Wave, QuickBooks, etc.)
- [ ] Set up expense categories (supplies, mileage, payroll, insurance)
- [ ] Never mix personal and business transactions
- [ ] Save 25–30% of profit for taxes in a separate savings account
