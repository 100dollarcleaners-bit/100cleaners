# Business Startup Checklist

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

## Phase 1 — Legal & Financial (Week 1)

- [ ] Form LLC and obtain EIN
- [ ] Open business bank account
- [ ] Purchase general liability insurance ($1M/$2M)
- [ ] Get local business license if required
- [ ] Set up basic accounting (Wave is free to start)
- [ ] Create business phone and email

## Phase 2 — Brand & Online Presence (Week 1–2)

- [ ] Choose company name and tagline
- [ ] Design logo (Canva or hire on Fiverr)
- [ ] Register domain and build simple website
- [ ] Set up Google Business Profile
- [ ] Create Yelp business page
- [ ] Create Instagram and Facebook business pages
- [ ] Join Nextdoor as a business
- [ ] Take 10–20 before/after photos for marketing

## Phase 3 — Operations Setup (Week 2)

- [ ] Purchase starter supply kit (see Product Shopping List)
- [ ] Buy uniform(s) — branded polo or all black
- [ ] Set pricing (see Pricing Guide — do NOT charge hourly)
- [ ] Create estimate/quote template
- [ ] Set up Stripe or Square for payments
- [ ] Print or digitize all SOP checklists
- [ ] Create customer intake form
- [ ] Write cancellation policy

## Phase 4 — First Clients (Week 2–4)

- [ ] Offer 3–5 discounted "portfolio" cleans to friends/family for photos & reviews
- [ ] Ask every happy client for a Google and Yelp review
- [ ] Post before/after content on social media
- [ ] Launch referral program ($25 off for referrer + referee)
- [ ] Respond to every inquiry within 1 hour

## Phase 5 — Scale Readiness (Month 2+)

- [ ] Document every process as an SOP
- [ ] Track time per job to validate pricing
- [ ] Set profit targets (aim for $50+ per regular clean minimum)
- [ ] Build lead list / CRM (spreadsheet or free CRM)
- [ ] Plan first hire when consistently booked 4+ days/week

## Startup Budget Snapshot

| Category | Estimated Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| LLC + licenses | $100–$500 |
| Insurance (annual) | $400–$1,200 |
| Supplies & equipment | $300–$800 |
| Website & branding | $0–$500 |
| Uniforms | $50–$150 |
| Marketing (initial) | $0–$300 |
| **Total to launch** | **$850–$3,450** |

## Success Metrics — First 90 Days

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Completed cleans | 20+ |
| Google reviews | 10+ (4.8+ stars) |
| Repeat clients | 30% of bookings |
| Average job profit | $50+ per regular clean |
| Response time to leads | Under 1 hour |
