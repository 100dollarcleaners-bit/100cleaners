# Professional Pricing Guide

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

## The #1 Rule: Do NOT Charge Hourly

Hourly pricing punishes efficiency. The faster you get, the less you earn. Price by **job scope**, not time.

---

## Base Residential Pricing (Up to 3 Bedrooms)

| Service | Suggested Price | Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| Regular / Maintenance Clean | $150 | Recurring clients, already-maintained homes |
| Deep Clean | $300 | First-time, neglected, or quarterly detail |
| Move-Out Clean | $350–$500+ | Empty home, all appliances, cabinets |
| Airbnb Turnover | $125–$200 | Linens, restock, photo-ready in 2–3 hrs |

Adjust for your market. LA/major metros run higher; rural areas lower.

## Sample Profit Model

| Service | Customer Pays | Cleaner Pay | Your Profit |
|---------|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| Regular Clean | $150 | $100 | **$50** |
| Deep Clean | $300 | $200 | **$100** |

**Your profit covers:** taxes, insurance, supplies, marketing, scheduling, vehicle, and your salary as owner.

Adjust cleaner pay for local labor market. Never go below minimum wage equivalent.

---

## First-Time Client Strategy

1. Quote **deep clean** price for first visit (even if they want "regular")
2. Explain: "First visit establishes our standard; maintenance pricing applies after"
3. Book recurring at regular rate with 10% discount for weekly/biweekly
4. Never discount more than 15% — trains clients to expect low prices

## Red Flags — Walk Away or Charge More

- Hoarding / biohazard situations → refer out or 3x+ pricing
- Client wants hourly rate → educate or decline
- "My last cleaner charged $80" → explain your SOPs and quality difference
- No-show history → require deposit before booking
- More than 3 revisions to scope → send written quote and hold price 7 days
