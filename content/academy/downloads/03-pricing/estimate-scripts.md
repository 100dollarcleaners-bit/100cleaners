# Estimate Phone & Text Scripts

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

---

## Inbound Call — New Lead

**You:** Thank you for calling [Company Name], this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?

**Client:** I need a cleaning quote.

**You:** I'd be happy to help. Can I get your name and the address of the home?

*[Collect: name, address, phone, email]*

**You:** Great. How many bedrooms and bathrooms? Is this for a regular maintenance clean, a deep clean, or move-out?

*[Listen — if first time, recommend deep clean first]*

**You:** Do you have any pets? Any add-ons like inside the oven, refrigerator, laundry, or interior windows?

**You:** Based on what you've described, your [service type] would be **$[PRICE]**. That includes [list 3–4 key items]. We can get you scheduled as early as [DATE]. Does that work for you?

**If yes:** Perfect. I'll send a confirmation text with a link to pay a $25 deposit to hold your spot. What's the best email?

**If price objection:** I understand. Our pricing reflects fully insured, background-checked cleaners and a detailed checklist for every room. We don't cut corners on supplies or training. Would you like to start with a deep clean at $[PRICE] and move to maintenance pricing after?

**If not ready:** No problem. I'll email you a quote valid for 7 days. Can I follow up in a few days?

---

## Text — Sending a Quote

> Hi [Name]! This is [Your Name] from [Company Name]. Based on your [3 bed / 2 bath] home, your [Regular/Deep] clean is **$[PRICE]**. Includes: all rooms, kitchen, baths, floors, dusting, trash. Add-ons available. Ready to book? Reply YES and I'll send your deposit link. — [Company Name]

---

## Text — Deposit Link

> Hi [Name]! Here's your booking link to secure [DATE] at [TIME]: [LINK]. $25 deposit holds your spot — balance due on service day. Reply with any questions!

---

## Text — Follow-Up (No Response After 24 hrs)

> Hi [Name], just following up on your cleaning quote for $[PRICE]. We have openings this week — want me to hold a spot for you?

---

## Text — Follow-Up (No Response After 3 Days)

> Hi [Name], last check-in from [Company Name]. Your quote is valid through [DATE]. Happy to answer any questions. No pressure — we're here when you're ready!

---

## In-Person / Walk-Through Quote

**You:** Thanks for having me out. I'm going to walk through and note anything that affects pricing — pet hair, buildup, extra rooms, that kind of thing.

*[Walk through — take notes and photos with permission]*

**You:** Based on what I see, I recommend starting with a deep clean at **$[PRICE]**. After that, maintenance cleans would be **$[PRICE]** on a [weekly/biweekly] schedule. I'll email you a written quote today.

---

## Objection: "Can You Do It Cheaper?"

**You:** I want to make sure you get great value. We use professional-grade products, carry insurance, and follow a room-by-room checklist so nothing gets missed. I can adjust the scope — for example, skip add-ons or reduce frequency — but our base pricing reflects the quality we stand behind.

---

## Objection: "I Only Need Certain Rooms"

**You:** We price by full-home scope because our team allocates a full time block and brings all supplies. For partial cleans, our minimum is $[MINIMUM] for up to [X] rooms. Which rooms are priority?

---

## Closing Script

**You:** You're all set for [DAY] at [TIME]. You'll get a confirmation email and a reminder text the day before. Our cleaner will text you when they're on the way. Is there gate code, parking, or pet info we should know?

**Always collect:** Gate code, parking instructions, pet details, preferred products (if any), areas to skip, emergency contact.
