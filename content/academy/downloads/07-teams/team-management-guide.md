# Team Management Guide

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Managing cleaners is where most cleaning companies break — or scale. This guide covers the systems that keep teams accountable, clients happy, and your sanity intact.

---

## The Management Mindset

You are not managing "help." You are leading a **field operations team** that represents your brand inside private homes. Every system below exists to:

1. **Protect clients** — trust, security, consistency
2. **Protect cleaners** — clear expectations, fair pay, documentation
3. **Protect you** — liability, quality proof, dispute resolution

---

## GPS Tracking & Job Verification

### Why GPS Matters

- Confirms arrival and departure times
- Resolves "they never showed" disputes
- Identifies chronic lateness patterns
- Supports payroll accuracy for hourly employees

### Recommended Tools

| Tool | Best For | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Jobber | Scheduling + GPS | Popular with home service |
| Housecall Pro | All-in-one field service | Strong mobile app |
| Connecteam | Team communication + GPS | Budget-friendly |
| Google Maps Timeline | Free backup | Cleaner shares location manually |

### GPS Policy (Share With Team)

> "We use GPS check-ins for arrival and departure only — not continuous tracking off the clock. This protects you when clients claim you weren't there, and protects clients when we confirm service completion."

### Implementation

1. **Arrival check-in** — required within 5 minutes of scheduled start
2. **Departure check-out** — required before leaving property
3. **Flag exceptions** — no check-in after 15 min = auto-alert to manager
4. **Never use GPS punitively** for bathroom breaks or legitimate stops

## Photo Documentation

Photos are your #1 quality control and dispute resolution tool.

### Required Photos (Every Job)

| When | What to Capture |
|------|-----------------|
| Arrival | Front door or entry (proves you were there) |
| Issues found | Pre-existing damage, heavy soil, broken items |
| Completion | Kitchen, bathrooms, made beds, floors |
| Departure | Same angle as arrival (optional but powerful) |

### Photo Standards

- Well-lit, not blurry
- Upload to team chat or app within 30 minutes
- Never include client personal items identifiable in marketing
- Store for 90 days minimum (cloud folder by date/client)

### When Photos Save You

- Client claims bathroom wasn't cleaned — you have timestamped proof
- Cleaner says they finished — photos show missed areas for coaching
- Damage dispute — arrival photo shows pre-existing condition

**Use the [Photo Documentation Checklist](../05-checklists/photo-checklist.md) from Module 5.**

## Group Chats & Communication

### Structure

| Channel | Purpose | Who's In |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Team Operations (Slack/WhatsApp/iMessage) | Daily updates, coverage, supply alerts | All active cleaners + you |
| Management Only | HR, firings, client complaints | You + lead cleaners |
| Client-facing | Never mix with team chat | Separate business line/app |

### Group Chat Rules

1. **Professional tone always** — this is a work record
2. **No client bashing** — discuss issues privately with manager
3. **Coverage requests** — post by 6 PM day before when possible
4. **Photos go in chat** — not DMs to owner only (transparency)
5. **Response time** — managers reply within 2 hours during business hours

### Daily Check-In Template

```
📍 [Name] — Arrived [Client Last Name] [Time]
📍 [Name] — Completed [Client Last Name] [Time] — No issues
⚠️ [Name] — [Client] — Running 20 min late, notified client
```

### What NOT to Do

- Don't let group chat replace the scheduling app
- Don't discuss pay disputes publicly in group chat
- Don't add clients to team channels

## Call-Offs & Coverage Protocol

### Call-Off Policy

| Notice | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| 24+ hours | No penalty, help find coverage |
| Same day (before 7 AM) | Warning, document |
| No-show | Immediate suspension pending review |

### Call-Off Procedure (Cleaner)

1. Text **manager AND** group chat (not just a friend on the team)
2. Include: name, client affected, reason (brief), availability to help reschedule
3. Do not contact client to cancel unless manager assigns you to

### Coverage Procedure (Manager)

1. Check backup cleaner list by zone
2. Offer bonus ($20–$50) for same-day coverage
3. Notify client **before** original appointment time
4. Document in scheduling app
5. Log call-off in performance tracker

### Backup Cleaner System

## Scheduling Best Practices

### Route by Geography

- Cluster jobs by city/zip to reduce drive time
- Max 4–5 jobs per cleaner per day (residential)
- Buffer 30 minutes between jobs for drive + overrun

### Skill Matching

| Job Type | Assign To |
|----------|-----------|
| Regular recurring | Any trained cleaner |
| Deep clean | Experienced only (90+ days) |
| Move-out | Lead cleaner or paired team |
| Difficult client | Your best communicator |

### Avoid Burnout

- No more than 6 days/week regularly
- Rotate heavy deep cleans across team
- Give preferred clients as retention reward

## Conflict Resolution

### Cleaner vs. Client

1. Listen to both sides without assuming
2. Review photos and notes from job
3. Offer re-clean if legitimate miss (company cost)
4. Document in client file
5. Reassign if personality mismatch

### Cleaner vs. Cleaner

1. Private conversation first
2. Clarify roles (lead vs. helper on team jobs)
3. Separate on paired jobs if needed
4. Escalate to written warning if unprofessional

---

## Lead Cleaner Role

Promote your best performer to lead when you have 3+ cleaners.

**Lead cleaner responsibilities:**
- Train new hires on shadow days
- First call for coverage gaps in their zone
- Quality spot-checks on team jobs
- +$10–$15 per job or monthly stipend ($200–$400)

**Lead cleaner does NOT:**
- Hire/fire without your approval
- Change pricing or make client promises
- Access financial data
