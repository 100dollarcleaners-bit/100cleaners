# Interior Window Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Scope:** Interior reachable windows only — no ladders, no exterior, no second-story  
**Add-on price:** +$50 (all reachable windows in up to 3 bed home)  
**Time target:** 30–45 min whole home | 5–8 min per window

---

## Purpose

Clean windows brighten the entire home and are a high-visibility upsell. This SOP covers **interior glass you can reach safely from the floor or a standard step stool (max 2 steps).**

---

## Safety & Scope Limits

**In scope:**
- [ ] Interior glass panes reachable without ladder
- [ ] Window sills and tracks (interior)
- [ ] Sliding glass door interior (if reachable)

**Out of scope — do not attempt:**
- ❌ Exterior windows
- ❌ Second-story or high transom windows
- ❌ Window cleaning requiring ladder (refer to window specialist)
- ❌ Removing window screens for exterior wash (unless trained and in deep/move-out scope)
- ❌ Damaged or cracked glass — report, don't pressure

---

## Supplies

- [ ] Glass cleaner ( ammonia-free preferred for tinted film )
- [ ] Microfiber cloths — 2 per window (wet/clean + dry/polish)
- [ ] Small scrub pad or old toothbrush (tracks)
- [ ] Vacuum crevice tool (tracks)
- [ ] Step stool (stable, 2-step max)
- [ ] Plastic scraper (sticker residue on glass — test corner first)
- [ ] Drop cloth or towel for sills

---

## Window Cleaning Order (Per Window)

Work one window at a time, top to bottom:

### Step 1 — Sill & Track
- [ ] Vacuum track to remove dead bugs, dust, debris
- [ ] Scrub track with small brush and all-purpose cleaner
- [ ] Wipe sill dry — check for plant water stains

### Step 2 — Frame & Ledge
- [ ] Dry dust frame top and sides
- [ ] Damp-wipe interior frame and ledge
- [ ] Wipe lock hardware and handle

### Step 3 — Glass (The S Method)
- [ ] Remove loose dust with dry microfiber
- [ ] Spray glass cleaner on cloth (not heavily on glass — avoids drips on sills/walls)
- [ ] Wipe in **S-pattern** top to bottom, overlapping strokes
- [ ] Dry/polish with clean dry microfiber immediately
- [ ] Check from multiple angles for streaks

### Step 4 — Final Check
- [ ] No drips on walls or sills
- [ ] Lock and screen (if present) replaced properly
- [ ] Open and close window to verify operation (don't force stuck windows)

---

## Glass Technique Details

**Why spray the cloth, not the glass:**
- Prevents damage to painted sills and wall below
- Reduces inhalation of spray in small bathrooms
- Gives better control on mirrors and small panes

**Streak-free finish:**
- [ ] Use two cloths minimum — one applies, one buffs
- [ ] Buff in circular motion on final pass
- [ ] Clean on cloudy day or indirect light when possible — sun dries cleaner too fast and streaks

**Hard water spots (deep clean):**
- [ ] Apply vinegar solution or calcium remover to cloth only
- [ ] Spot treat, dwell 2 min, wipe and polish
- [ ] Test tinted windows in corner first

---

## Mirrors (Same Technique)

Bathrooms and closet mirrors use identical S-method:

- [ ] Spray cloth, not mirror
- [ ] Wipe top to bottom
- [ ] Polish dry — check from doorway sightline (client's view)

---

## Sliding Glass Doors

- [ ] Vacuum and clean bottom track thoroughly (deepest dirt collects here)
- [ ] Clean interior glass panel — both sides of interior-facing glass if reachable without going outside
- [ ] Wipe door handle and lock
- [ ] Time: 10–15 min per door

---

## Whole-Home Window Route

Efficient path for 3 bed home (~15–20 panes):

- [ ] Start upstairs (if applicable) — back rooms to stairs
- [ ] Main floor — bedrooms, living, dining, kitchen pass-throughs
- [ ] Bathrooms last (steam may re-fog if baths just cleaned — do windows before bath wet work OR return to buff)
- [ ] Kitchen window after stovetop work (grease mist lands on glass)

**Pro tip:** On deep cleans, do windows **after** dusting but **before** floor mop (sill debris falls to floor).

---

## Quality Standards

Windows pass when:

- [ ] Glass is clear with no streaks, smudges, or fingerprints at normal viewing distance
- [ ] Sills and tracks are debris-free
- [ ] No cleaner residue on frames
- [ ] Client can see through glass from main seating area

---

## Do NOT (Windows)

- ❌ Lean out of windows to reach exterior — ever
- ❌ Use razor blades on tinted, coated, or Low-E glass
- ❌ Use abrasive pads on glass
- ❌ Clean windows in direct hot sun — streak city
- ❌ Soak untreated wood sills
- ❌ Remove screens you can't reinstall flush
- ❌ Guarantee removal of construction paint overspray — refer to specialist
