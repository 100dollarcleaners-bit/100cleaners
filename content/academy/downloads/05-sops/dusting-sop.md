# Dusting SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Applies to:** All services — regular, deep, move-out, STR  
**Time target:** Regular 5–10 min/room | Deep 15–20 min/room (includes fans, vents, baseboards)

---

## Purpose

Dusting removes allergens and gives rooms a polished look. Always work **top to bottom** so falling dust lands on surfaces you haven't cleaned yet. Use dry microfiber first; damp only when needed.

---

## Supplies

- [ ] Microfiber cloths (dry — 2 per room minimum)
- [ ] Extendable duster (ceiling fans, high corners)
- [ ] Lambswool or microfiber duster (fragile items)
- [ ] Step stool (safe, non-slip)
- [ ] Vacuum with brush attachment (vents, upholstery edges)
- [ ] Furniture polish (wood only — sparingly, if client requests)
- [ ] Glass cleaner (for framed art glass only)

---

## Top-to-Bottom Dusting Order

In every room, follow this sequence:

1. [ ] Ceiling corners and cobwebs
2. [ ] Ceiling fans and light fixtures
3. [ ] Vents and return air grilles
4. [ ] Top of door frames and window frames
5. [ ] Wall art, mirrors, clocks (dry dust frame first)
6. [ ] Shelves — top shelf down, back to front
7. [ ] Furniture surfaces (dressers, nightstands, TV stands)
8. [ ] Lamps, décor, electronics (dry dust only on screens)
9. [ ] Chair rails, window sills
10. [ ] Baseboards (see Deep Clean SOP for damp-wipe)
11. [ ] Floor vacuum (captures fallen dust)

---

## Microfiber Technique

**The 8-fold method** — maximize one cloth per room:

- [ ] Fold cloth into quarters (8 usable sides)
- [ ] Use one clean side per surface type or zone
- [ ] Flip to fresh side when cloth looks gray or feels damp
- [ ] Never reuse a bathroom cloth in living areas
- [ ] Shake cloth outside or into trash between rooms — don't spread dust indoors

**Dry dusting:** Default for all surfaces. Captures dust without streaking.

**Damp dusting:** Lightly mist cloth with all-purpose cleaner for sticky surfaces (kitchen shelves, TV stands with grease). Wring nearly dry.

---

## Ceiling Fans

**Regular clean:** Wipe accessible blades with damp microfiber on extendable duster.

**Deep clean / add-on (+$10 each):**

- [ ] Turn fan OFF — verify switch, not just remote
- [ ] Place sheet or towel under fan to catch debris
- [ ] Dust blade tops first (where dust hides), then bottoms
- [ ] Wipe motor housing
- [ ] Wipe pull chains and light fixture
- [ ] Vacuum fallen dust from floor below

**Do NOT** spin blades dry — spreads dust everywhere.

---

## Vents & Return Grilles

- [ ] Vacuum with brush attachment first (loosens dust)
- [ ] Wipe slats with damp microfiber wrapped around knife (protected) or vent brush
- [ ] Do not remove vent covers unless in move-out scope and you can reinstall
- [ ] Deep clean: wipe wall surrounding vent (dust halo)

---

## Electronics & Fragile Items

- [ ] TV screens: dry microfiber only — no spray directly on screen
- [ ] Dust remotes, game controllers (high-touch — disinfect if client requests)
- [ ] Pick up and dust **under** small items, then replace exactly where found
- [ ] Do not dust collections of fragile valuables without moving carefully one at a time
- [ ] Never spray polish on electronics

---

## Blinds & Window Treatments

- [ ] Close blinds; dust each slat top to bottom with damp cloth or blind duster
- [ ] Reverse direction; dust other side
- [ ] Curtains: vacuum with upholstery attachment (light touch)
- [ ] Do not wash curtains unless explicit add-on

---

## Furniture & Wood Surfaces

- [ ] Dust with dry microfiber following wood grain
- [ ] Polish only if client requests and product is approved (avoid buildup on regular cleans)
- [ ] Lift items to dust underneath — photo placement if valuable
- [ ] Don't dust around clutter piles — straighten lightly, dust, replace

---

## High-Touch Points (Include in Every Dust Pass)

- [ ] Door handles and knobs
- [ ] Light switches and switch plates
- [ ] Handrails on stairs
- [ ] Cabinet handles (kitchen and bath)

---

## Quality Standards

Dusting passes when:

- [ ] No visible dust on surfaces at normal viewing height
- [ ] Ceiling fan blades show no fuzzy buildup (deep clean)
- [ ] Vents are not gray with dust halo
- [ ] Shelves pass the "white glove swipe" on deep cleans
- [ ] No streaks on wood or glass from damp dusting

---

## Do NOT (Dusting)

- ❌ Dust bottom to top — you'll redo everything
- ❌ Use feather dusters as primary tool — they spread dust
- ❌ Spray furniture polish on every surface every visit — creates waxy buildup
- ❌ Climb unstable furniture to reach fans — use proper step stool
- ❌ Dust computer keyboards with wet cloth
- ❌ Move framed photos without supporting both sides
- ❌ Ignore cobwebs in ceiling corners — clients see them from the couch

---

## Time-Saving Tips for Field Teams

- Carry extendable duster on shoulder between rooms — hit fans and corners first in every room before surface work
- One dry cloth + one damp cloth per caddy — swap per room
- Deep clean day: dust entire home before any wet work begins
