# Move-Out Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Service type:** Vacant home turnover for landlord, realtor, or seller  
**Target home:** Empty — no furniture, no personal belongings  
**Price reference:** $350–$500+ (size and condition dependent)  
**Time target:** 5–8 hours (solo) | 4–6 hours (2-person team)

---

## Purpose

Deliver a **move-in ready** empty home. Every room, cabinet, closet, and appliance must pass a white-glove inspection. This is the highest-detail residential service you offer.

---

## Pre-Job Requirements

**Confirm before booking:**

- [ ] Home is 100% vacant (no furniture, boxes, or stored items)
- [ ] Utilities are ON (water, electricity, HVAC)
- [ ] Scope includes all appliances, cabinets, closets, and garage (if applicable)
- [ ] Keys or lockbox access confirmed
- [ ] Photos required for landlord/realtor sign-off

**Do NOT start** if belongings remain — charge a partial clean or reschedule.

---

## Supplies Checklist

Full deep clean kit, plus:

- [ ] Oven cleaner + heavy degreaser
- [ ] Refrigerator cleaner (baking soda solution or approved product)
- [ ] Cabinet shelf liner remover / adhesive remover
- [ ] Step stool and flashlight (inside cabinets, closets)
- [ ] Vacuum with hose, crevice, and upholstery tools
- [ ] Steam cleaner (optional — bathrooms, tile)
- [ ] Razor scraper (stove glass, sticker residue — test first)
- [ ] Multiple trash bags (clients often leave debris)
- [ ] Extension duster
- [ ] Grout brush, toilet brush, detail brushes

---

## Move-Out Room Order

Work top to bottom, back of house to front, wet areas with dwell time first.

| Order | Area | Time Target |

| 3 | All bedrooms + closets (inside/out) | 30–40 min each |
| 4 | Living, dining, office, hallways | 30 min each |

## Kitchen — Move-Out Standard

### Cabinets & Drawers
- [ ] Empty check — remove any leftover items to trash (document if excessive)
- [ ] Wipe inside every shelf, drawer, and door panel
- [ ] Clean handles, hinges, and frame edges
- [ ] Remove shelf liner if peeling; wipe adhesive residue

### Appliances
- [ ] **Refrigerator:** Remove shelves/drawers, wash, wipe interior walls, clean exterior and handles, pull forward and clean floor underneath if accessible
- [ ] **Oven:** Remove racks, degrease interior, clean glass door inside/out, wipe stovetop and control panel
- [ ] **Microwave:** Clean interior and exterior
- [ ] **Dishwasher:** Wipe interior, filter area, door edges, exterior
- [ ] **Range hood:** Degrease filter (if removable and in scope) and exterior

### Surfaces
- [ ] Degrease backsplash and all counter surfaces
- [ ] Clean sink, faucet, and disposal splash area
- [ ] Wipe all cabinet fronts
- [ ] Clean inside of pantry/closet if present

*Full procedure: see Kitchen SOP*

---

## Bathroom — Move-Out Standard

- [ ] Scrub tub/shower — remove all soap scum and mildew stains
- [ ] Grout detail where discolored
- [ ] Toilet: inside bowl, tank exterior, seat, base, behind toilet, floor bolts
- [ ] Vanity: inside drawers and cabinets (empty), counter, sink, faucet
- [ ] Mirror, light fixtures, exhaust fan cover (exterior wipe)
- [ ] Towel bars, toilet paper holder, hooks
- [ ] Floor: sweep, mop, edges

*Full procedure: see Bathroom SOP*

---

## Bedrooms & Closets

- [ ] Wipe inside all closets — shelves, rods, floor, doors inside/out
- [ ] Clean closet tracks and door frames
- [ ] Wipe all walls for scuffs (magic eraser — test first)
- [ ] Dust ceiling fan, vents, light fixtures
- [ ] Clean window sills and tracks
- [ ] Baseboards and door frames
- [ ] Vacuum/mop floor including closet floors

---

## Living Areas & Hallways

- [ ] All vents and returns wiped
- [ ] Light fixtures and ceiling fans dusted
- [ ] Fireplace surround wiped (no chimney work)
- [ ] All interior doors wiped both sides + frames
- [ ] Switch plates and outlet covers wiped
- [ ] Stair rails and spindles
- [ ] Baseboards throughout

---

## Laundry Room

- [ ] Wipe washer and dryer exterior
- [ ] Clean inside washer door gasket (front-load)
- [ ] Wipe shelves, sink, and counter
- [ ] Sweep and mop floor
- [ ] Clean lint trap area exterior

---

## Quality Standards (Landlord/Realtor Pass)

- [ ] Open any cabinet — no crumbs, dust, or sticky residue
- [ ] Open oven and fridge — no odor, no grease, shelves reinstalled
- [ ] Bathrooms pass a "would I move in?" test
- [ ] Closets are empty, wiped, and floor is clean
- [ ] All floors vacuumed at edges and mopped
- [ ] No cobwebs, no dust on fans/vents
- [ ] Photos document kitchen, each bath, and main living areas

---

## Do NOT (Move-Out)

- ❌ Clean a partially occupied home at move-out price
- ❌ Dispose of large abandoned items without written approval
- ❌ Paint, patch walls, or repair fixtures — cleaning only
- ❌ Remove permanent fixtures or appliance parts you can't reinstall
- ❌ Guarantee mold remediation or pest removal
- ❌ Leave wet cabinets closed — air dry before closing doors
- ❌ Skip the fridge pull-out — landlords always check

---

## Documentation Checklist

- [ ] Before photos (if access allows pre-clean)
- [ ] After photos: kitchen (oven/fridge open), each bathroom, master closet, living area
- [ ] Note any damage, missing hardware, or issues beyond cleaning scope
- [ ] Send completion report to property manager within 2 hours
