# Laundry Add-On SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Service type:** Wash / dry / fold add-on  
**Price reference:** +$50 per load (one load = one standard washer capacity)  
**Time target:** 60–90 min total (includes machine cycle wait time — work other rooms while machines run)

---

## Purpose

When a client purchases the laundry add-on, your team washes, dries, and folds **one load** of linens or clothing per the client's instructions. This SOP ensures fabric care, prevents dye disasters, and delivers hotel-quality folds.

**Confirm on work order before starting:** One load or multiple? Linens only or clothing? Special detergent (hypoallergenic, scent-free)?

---

## Pre-Job Confirmation

Ask or verify in booking notes:

- [ ] **One load (+$50)** or additional loads (+$50 each)?
- [ ] Linens and towels only, or clothing included?
- [ ] Washer/dryer location accessible?
- [ ] Client detergent or use yours (bring hypoallergenic backup)?
- [ ] Hang vs fold preferences for specific items?
- [ ] Any "do not wash" items in the hamper (dry-clean only)?

**Do NOT** wash items without checking labels if mixed load.

---

## Supplies

- [ ] Hypoallergenic detergent (unscented backup)
- [ ] Dryer sheets or wool dryer balls (if client approves)
- [ ] Laundry bag for transport (STR turnovers)
- [ ] Stain pretreatment stick (spot use only)
- [ ] Hangers (only if client provides and requests)
- [ ] Gloves (optional — for dirty STR linens)

---

## Wash / Dry / Fold Protocol

### Step 1 — Sort & Inspect (5 min)

- [ ] Separate whites, darks, and colors if mixed — **when in doubt, separate**
- [ ] Check pockets (tissue, phones, money — return to client)
- [ ] Read care labels on clothing items
- [ ] Remove dry-clean-only items — leave on counter with note
- [ ] Pretreat visible stains only — blot, don't guarantee removal
- [ ] Shake out excess debris (pet hair, sand) before loading

**Do NOT wash:** Wool, silk, dry-clean only, items labeled hand wash (unless client explicitly approves and accepts risk).

---

### Step 2 — Wash (30–40 min machine time)

- [ ] Load washer — **do not overfill** (3/4 full max for proper agitation)
- [ ] Select appropriate cycle:
  - [ ] **Linens/towels:** Warm or hot (if fabric allows), normal cycle
  - [ ] **Clothing:** Cold or warm per label, normal or delicate
  - [ ] **Delicates:** Delicate cycle, cold, mesh bag if available
- [ ] Use correct detergent amount — too much leaves residue
- [ ] Start washer; **note finish time** on phone
- [ ] Proceed to clean other rooms while waiting — do not stand idle

---

### Step 3 — Dry (30–50 min machine time)

- [ ] Transfer promptly when wash completes — mildew starts in 30 min sitting wet
- [ ] Clean dryer lint trap **before every load** — fire safety requirement
- [ ] Select heat setting:
  - [ ] **Towels/linens:** Medium or high (if cotton)
  - [ ] **Clothing:** Low or per label
  - [ ] **Delicates:** Low heat or air dry
- [ ] Do not over-dry — removes softness from fabrics
- [ ] Continue other cleaning tasks while drying

---

### Step 4 — Fold & Stage (15–20 min)

Fold to **100% Cleaner standard:**

**Towels:**
- [ ] Bath towels: fold in thirds lengthwise, then in half — smooth edge out
- [ ] Hand towels and washcloths: fold in thirds, stack matching sizes

**Bed linens:**
- [ ] Fitted sheet: tuck corners into each other, fold flat stack
- [ ] Flat sheet and pillowcases: fold crisp rectangle, stack as a set
- [ ] Place on clean bed or designated shelf — never floor

**Clothing:**
- [ ] Fold shirts, pants, and casual wear neatly
- [ ] Hang dress shirts, blouses, and items client specifies on **client hangers only**
- [ ] Match socks in pairs; fold underwear in drawer-ready stacks
- [ ] Place on bed or counter — **never mix with dirty items**

---

## STR / Airbnb Laundry Workflow

For turnovers with in-unit laundry:

- [ ] Strip beds first → start wash immediately on arrival
- [ ] Use host-provided detergent and settings from host guide
- [ ] Time laundry to finish before bed-making phase (~90 min into turnover)
- [ ] If laundry won't finish in 2–3 hr window: notify host, use backup linen set if available
- [ ] Never leave wet laundry in washer when locking up

| Loads | Price | Time Impact |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 load | +$50 | Work other rooms during cycles |
| 2 loads | +$100 | May need extended time — confirm schedule |
| 3+ loads | Custom quote | Often requires 2nd cleaner or return trip |

Run loads **sequentially** unless client has two machines. Do not leave washer unattended off-site.

---

## Quality Standards

Laundry add-on passes when:

- [ ] Items are clean, dry, and smell fresh (not mildewy or overly perfumed)
- [ ] Folds are neat and uniform
- [ ] No dye transfer between items
- [ ] Items placed where client requested (bed, basket, closet)
- [ ] Lint trap cleaned before leaving
- [ ] Washer door left open to air (front-load — prevents mold)

---

## Do NOT (Laundry)

- ❌ Mix reds with whites — ever
- ❌ Wash client dry-clean items to save time
- ❌ Use bleach without explicit client approval
- ❌ Leave wet laundry in washer when job ends
- ❌ Take client laundry off-site to your home machines
- ❌ Use heavily scented detergent unless client requests
- ❌ Overload washer to finish faster — items won't get clean
- ❌ Ignore "delicate" labels on expensive clothing

---

## Damage Protocol

If something goes wrong:

1. Stop immediately
2. Photo the item and machine settings
3. Notify client and office same day
4. Do not discard damaged items
5. Document in job notes — shrinkage, dye bleed, or machine error
