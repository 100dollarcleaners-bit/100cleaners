# Deep Cleaning SOP

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

**Service type:** Top-to-bottom detail clean  
**Target home:** Up to 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms  
**Price reference:** $300  
**Time target:** 4–6 hours (solo) | 3–4 hours (2-person team)

---

## Purpose

Remove accumulated grime, buildup, and neglected areas. Deep cleans are for first-time clients, quarterly refreshes, pre-event prep, or homes that haven't been professionally cleaned in 60+ days.

**Regular clean ($150) maintains. Deep clean ($300) restores.**

---

## Supplies Checklist

Everything from Regular Clean, plus:

- [ ] Degreaser (kitchen)
- [ ] Calcium / lime remover (showers, faucets)
- [ ] Grout brush or old toothbrush
- [ ] Magic eraser or melamine sponge (spot test first)
- [ ] Extendable duster + step stool
- [ ] Steam cleaner (if equipped and trained — tile, grout, appliances)
- [ ] Baseboard brush or flat mop edge tool
- [ ] Vacuum crevice tool
- [ ] Plastic scraper (sticker/gunk removal)
- [ ] Heavy-duty bathroom scrubber

---

## Pre-Job Setup (10 minutes)

- [ ] Confirm deep clean scope on work order (oven, fridge, baseboards, fans?)
- [ ] Inspect home; adjust time estimate if home exceeds 3 bed / heavy buildup
- [ ] Take before photos
- [ ] Identify surfaces that cannot get wet (unsealed wood, electronics)
- [ ] Pre-treat heavy areas (oven, shower) and let product dwell while you work elsewhere

---

## Deep Clean Sequence

| Phase | Area | Time Target |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Ceiling fans, vents, cobwebs (top of home) | 30–45 min |
| 2 | Bedrooms — detail dust + baseboards | 25 min each |
| 3 | Living / dining — detail dust + baseboards | 30 min |
| 4 | Bathrooms — full disinfect + grout/scum | 35–45 min each |
| 5 | Kitchen — degrease + detail all surfaces | 45–60 min |
| 6 | Baseboards — whole home | 30–45 min |
| 7 | Interior windows (if in scope) | 30 min |
| 8 | Floors — vacuum, edges, mop | 30–45 min |
| 9 | Final inspection | 10 min |

---

## Top-to-Bottom Rule

Always work **high to low** in each room:

- [ ] Ceiling corners and cobwebs
- [ ] Ceiling fans and light fixtures (dust/blades)
- [ ] Vents and return air grilles (wipe exterior)
- [ ] Wall art, shelves, ledges
- [ ] Furniture surfaces
- [ ] Baseboards
- [ ] Floor last

*Full dusting technique: see Dusting SOP*

---

## Baseboards (Whole Home)

- [ ] Dry dust first with microfiber or vacuum brush attachment
- [ ] Damp-wipe with all-purpose cleaner on a flat pad
- [ ] Pay extra attention to kitchen, bathrooms, and entryways
- [ ] Do not soak baseboards — wring cloth well on painted surfaces

---

## Kitchen — Deep Scope

- [ ] Degrease range hood, backsplash, and stove area
- [ ] Clean stovetop burners/grates (remove if safe, soak if needed)
- [ ] Wipe all cabinet fronts, handles, and drawer pulls
- [ ] Clean inside microwave
- [ ] Detail sink, faucet, and disposal area
- [ ] Wipe small appliances on counter
- [ ] **Add-ons:** Inside oven (+$40), inside refrigerator (+$35)

*Full procedure: see Kitchen SOP*

---

## Bathroom — Deep Scope

- [ ] Heavy soap scum removal on shower/tub (dwell time with appropriate product)
- [ ] Grout scrub where discolored
- [ ] Descale faucets and showerheads (exterior)
- [ ] Full toilet detail including base bolts and behind seat
- [ ] Clean inside vanity drawers **only if empty and in scope**
- [ ] Polish mirrors and chrome

*Full procedure: see Bathroom SOP*

---

## Steam Cleaning (Where Applicable)

Use steam only if trained and client surfaces allow:

- [ ] Tile and grout (not unsealed natural stone)
- [ ] Shower glass (follow with squeegee)
- [ ] Stovetop grease (after degreaser pre-treat)
- [ ] Sanitize bathroom fixtures

**Do NOT steam:** unsealed wood, laminate with damaged seams, electronics, painted surfaces that peel, or areas with loose grout.

---

## Buildup Targets (Deep Clean Only)

- [ ] Fingerprints on doors and switch plates (whole home)
- [ ] Door frames and tops of doors
- [ ] Window sills and tracks (reachable interior)
- [ ] Under furniture edges (vacuum, don't move heavy furniture alone)
- [ ] Light switch plates and outlet covers (wipe, don't remove)
- [ ] Pet hair on furniture and baseboards

---

## Quality Standards

Deep clean passes when:

- [ ] No visible dust on fans, vents, or high ledges
- [ ] Baseboards are clean to the touch (no gray film)
- [ ] Kitchen is degreased — no sticky residue on range area
- [ ] Bathrooms: grout and glass show significant improvement
- [ ] All regular-clean standards are also met
- [ ] Client could photograph any room for a listing

---

## Do NOT (Deep Clean)

- ❌ Promise same-day deep clean for 4+ bed homes without a walk-through
- ❌ Use abrasive pads on stainless steel, glass, or marble
- ❌ Steam without testing a hidden spot first
- ❌ Move heavy furniture alone — team lift or skip
- ❌ Strip wax or refinish floors — refer to specialist
- ❌ Clean mold larger than a postage stamp — document and notify client
- ❌ Rush baseboards — they define "deep" vs "regular" in the client's eyes
