# Cleaner Interview Questions & Scoring Rubric

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Use this guide to run consistent, professional interviews for residential cleaning candidates. Score each answer 1–5 and total at the end. **Minimum passing score: 70/100** (adjust for your standards).

---

## How to Use This Guide

1. **Phone screen first** (10–15 min) — filter before in-person or working interview
2. **In-person or video interview** (30–45 min) — use questions below
3. **Paid trial clean** (2–3 hours) — observe quality, speed, communication, and attitude
4. Score immediately after each stage while notes are fresh

### Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Excellent — exceeds expectations, specific examples |
| 4 | Good — solid answer, minor gaps |
| 3 | Acceptable — adequate but generic |
| 2 | Weak — red flags or vague responses |
| 1 | Poor — disqualifying or dishonest |

---

## Section 1: Experience & Skills (25 points)

### 1. Tell me about your cleaning experience. What types of homes have you cleaned?

**What you're listening for:** Residential experience, variety of home sizes, honesty about skill level.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2+ years residential, specific examples (deep cleans, move-outs, recurring) |
| 3 | Some experience, mostly commercial or personal only |
| 1 | No experience and unwilling to learn |

---

### 2. Walk me through how you would clean a bathroom from start to finish.

**What you're listening for:** Systematic approach — top to bottom, disinfect high-touch areas, proper product use.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Clear sequence: dust/vents → surfaces → toilet → shower/tub → mirrors → floor last |
| 3 | General steps but misses disinfection or order |
| 1 | No logical process |

---

### 3. What's the difference between a regular maintenance clean and a deep clean?

**What you're listening for:** Understanding of scope — baseboards, inside appliances, detail work vs. surface maintenance.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Names 5+ deep-clean tasks not in regular cleans |
| 3 | Knows deep is "more thorough" but can't specify |
| 1 | No distinction |

---

### 4. How do you handle pet hair, heavy grease, or soap scum buildup?

**What you're listening for:** Product knowledge, patience, technique — not just "scrub harder."

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Mentions degreasers, enzyme cleaners, microfiber, dwell time, tools |
| 3 | Willing to tackle but limited technique |
| 1 | Avoids difficult jobs |

---

### 5. Are you comfortable with laundry, making beds, and light organizing?

**What you're listening for:** Flexibility for add-on services common in residential cleaning.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Comfortable with all; gives examples |
| 3 | Some tasks only |
| 1 | Refuses common add-ons |

## Section 2: Reliability & Work Ethic (25 points)

| Score | Criteria |

| Score | Criteria |

| 1 | No-showed or last-minute pattern |

| Score | Criteria |

| 1 | No transportation |

### 10. How do you stay motivated during repetitive work?

## Red Flags — Do Not Hire

- Refuses background check without valid legal reason
- Badmouths every former employer
- Cannot explain basic cleaning sequence
- Shows up late to interview without notice
- Trial clean quality below your minimum standard
- Discusses stealing clients or working "on the side"
- Unwilling to follow company SOPs or use required products
