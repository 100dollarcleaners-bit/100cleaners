# Review Request Templates

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Reviews are your most valuable marketing asset. Use these templates after confirmed satisfaction — never before you've verified the client is happy.

---

## When to Ask

| Timing | Effectiveness |
|--------|---------------|
| Client compliments you in person | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Ask immediately |
| Positive reply to quality-check text | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Same day |
| 24 hours after service | ⭐⭐⭐ Standard |
| Before verifying satisfaction | ❌ Never |

**Golden rule:** "Anything we missed?" → wait for positive response → then ask for review.

## Google Review Templates

### Google — Text Message (Primary)

```
Hi [Name]! So glad you're happy with [Company]. If you have 60 seconds, a
Google review helps other [City] families find trustworthy cleaners:

[Your Google Review Link]

Thank you! — [Your Name]
```

### Google — Email

**Subject:** Quick favor? ⭐

```
Hi [First Name],

Thank you for choosing [Company] for your home in [City/Neighborhood]. We
hope today's [regular/deep] clean gave you one less thing to worry about.

If we earned it, would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It takes about
60 seconds and helps local families find reliable cleaning service.

→ Leave a Google Review: [Google Review Link]

We read every review personally. If anything wasn't perfect, reply to this
email first — we'll make it right before you post anywhere.

With gratitude,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]
[Phone]
```

### Google — Follow-Up (7 Days, No Review Yet)

**Subject:** Still happy with your clean?

```
Hi [First Name],

Just checking in — hope the house still feels great after our visit on [Date].

No pressure at all, but if you meant to leave a review and life got busy,
here's the link again: [Google Review Link]

Either way, we're here for your next clean!

[Your Name]
```

### Google — In-Person Script

```
"We really appreciate clients like you. If you ever have a minute, Google
reviews help small businesses like ours more than you might think. I'd be
grateful if you could share your experience — I can text you the link right now."
```

## Yelp Review Templates

### Yelp — Text Message

```
Hi [Name]! Thank you for trusting [Company]. If your experience was great,
a Yelp review helps other [City] homeowners find us:

[Your Yelp Review Link]

We appreciate you! — [Your Name]
```

### Yelp — Email

**Subject:** Your feedback on Yelp?

```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks again for choosing [Company] for your [service type] on [Date].

Yelp is where many [City] families search for cleaners. If we exceeded your
expectations, a honest review would mean the world to our small team:

→ Review us on Yelp: [Yelp Review Link]

Please note: Yelp has policies against incentivized reviews, so this is
simply a genuine request from a grateful business owner — no strings attached.

If something wasn't right, please email me directly at [email] before posting.
We'll fix it.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]
```

### Yelp — Post-Recurring Milestone (10th Clean)

```
Hi [Name]! You've been with [Company] for [X] cleans now — thank you for
your loyalty. If Yelp has been part of how you found us, a review sharing
your long-term experience would help other [City] families:

[Yelp Review Link]

— [Your Name]
```

## Multi-Platform Request (Choose One)

**Don't ask for Google AND Yelp in the same message** — pick the platform where you need reviews most.

### Priority Guide

| Situation | Ask For |
|-----------|---------|
| Need local SEO / Google Maps ranking | Google |
| Strong Yelp market (SF, LA, NYC, etc.) | Yelp |
| Client found you on Yelp | Yelp |
| Client found you on Google | Google |
| Under 10 Google reviews | Google |
| Under 10 Yelp reviews | Yelp |

## Review Response Templates

### Google/Yelp — 5-Star Response

```
Thank you so much, [Name]! We loved caring for your home and look forward
to your next visit. — [Your Name], [Company]
```

### Google/Yelp — 4-Star Response

```
Thank you, [Name]! We're glad you're happy. If there's anything we can do
to earn that fifth star next time, let us know — we want every visit to be
perfect. — [Your Name]
```

### Google/Yelp — 1–3 Star Response

```
[Name], thank you for the honest feedback. I'm sorry we fell short. I'd like
to discuss this personally — please call/text me at [phone]. We want to make
this right. — [Your Name], Owner
```

## What NOT to Do

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Offer discount for review | Google/Yelp may remove reviews; TOS violation |
| Ask only happy clients (review gating) | FTC concerns; platform penalties |
| Employee reviews | Prohibited; account risk |
| Fake reviews | Legal and platform penalties |
| Same IP as business | Reviews filtered |

## Review Link Setup

### Google Review Link

1. Google Business Profile → Home → "Get more reviews"
2. Copy short link (e.g., g.page/r/...)
3. Save in CRM template

### Yelp Review Link

1. Yelp for Business → Grow → Share review request
2. Copy direct link
3. Test on your phone before sending
