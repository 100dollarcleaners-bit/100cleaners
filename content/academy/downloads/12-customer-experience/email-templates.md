# Email Templates for Cleaning Companies

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Professional emails build trust before you ever enter a client's home. Replace bracketed fields with your details. Send from a branded address (hello@yourcompany.com), not a personal Gmail.

---

## 1. Welcome Email — New Client

**Subject:** Welcome to [Company Name]! 🏠

```
Hi [First Name],

Welcome to [Company Name] — and thank you for trusting us with your home.

Here's what to expect:

📅 Your first clean: [Day, Date] at [Time]
📍 Address: [Full Address]
🧹 Service: [Regular / Deep / Move-Out] — $[Price]
👤 Your cleaner: [Name] (when available)

BEFORE WE ARRIVE
• Ensure we have access (key, code, or be home)
• Secure pets if needed
• Note any fragile or priority areas in reply to this email

PAYMENT
• Card on file ending [XXXX]
• [Or: Balance of $[Amount] due after service]

QUESTIONS?
Reply to this email or text [phone].

We're excited to serve you!

[Your Name]
[Company Name]
[Phone] | [Website]
```

## 9. Service Recovery / Apology

**Subject:** We want to make this right — [Company Name]

```
Hi [First Name],

I'm [Your Name], owner of [Company Name]. I understand your recent clean
didn't meet our standards, and I sincerely apologize.

I've reviewed your service notes and would like to offer a complimentary
re-clean on [Proposed Date/Time], focusing on [specific areas].

If that doesn't work, reply with a better time or call me directly at
[phone]. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Thank you for giving us the chance to fix this.

[Your Name]
Owner, [Company Name]
```

## Email Best Practices

| Practice | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Subject lines | Specific, under 50 characters |
| Send time | Tue–Thu, 9–11 AM local |
| Mobile | Short paragraphs, bullet points |
| Signature | Logo, phone, website, review link |
| Compliance | Include unsubscribe for marketing emails |
| Attachments | Cancellation policy on first booking |
