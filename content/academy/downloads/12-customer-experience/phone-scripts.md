# Phone Scripts for Cleaning Companies

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Answer every call live when possible. These scripts keep conversations professional, move toward booking, and filter bad-fit clients. Customize with your company name, prices, and policies.

---

## Script 1: Incoming Call — General Inquiry

**Goal:** Qualify, quote, book.

```
"Thank you for calling [Company Name], this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?"

[Listen]

"Great — I'd love to get you a quote. Can I ask a few quick questions?"

1. "What city is the home located in?"
2. "How many bedrooms and bathrooms?"
3. "Is this a regular maintenance clean, deep clean, or move-out?"
4. "How often are you looking for service — one-time or recurring?"
5. "Do you have any pets?"
6. "When were you hoping to get on the schedule?"

"Based on what you've described, you're looking at approximately $[Price] for a
[service type]. That includes [list 2-3 highlights: all supplies, background-
checked cleaners, satisfaction guarantee]."

"Would you like to book? I have [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] available."

[If yes → collect name, address, email, card on file or deposit]
[If hesitant → "I can text you a quote and booking link right now."]
```

## Script 2: Price Shopper / "How Much Do You Charge?"

**Goal:** Give range, anchor value, avoid dead-end.

```
"I'd be happy to help with pricing. It depends on the size and condition of the
home. Most of our [regular/deep] cleans for a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home run
between $[Low] and $[High]."

"What I can do is ask two quick questions and give you an exact quote — takes
about 60 seconds. Fair enough?"

[Qualify → quote → book]

"We're not the cheapest in [City], and that's intentional — we pay our cleaners
well, carry insurance, and guarantee the work. If you'd like the lowest price,
I can refer you to a marketplace. If you want it done right, we're a great fit."
```

---

## Script 3: Recurring Service Inquiry

**Goal:** Sell bi-weekly, lock in card on file.

```
"For recurring clients we typically recommend bi-weekly — it keeps the home
manageable and saves you from playing catch-up."

"Our rate for a [X] bed / [X] bath on bi-weekly is $[Price] per visit. We assign
the same cleaner whenever possible so they learn your preferences."

"We keep a card on file and charge after each visit — no hassle. You can skip or
reschedule with 48 hours notice."

"Want me to get you on the schedule for a first clean this [Day]?"
```

---

## Script 4: Deep Clean / First-Time Client

**Goal:** Set expectations, collect deposit.

```
"For first-time or deep cleans, we do a top-to-bottom detail — baseboards,
vents, inside appliances if selected, extra attention in bathrooms and kitchen."

"For your home, that's $[Price]. It typically takes our team [X] hours."

"We do require a 50% deposit to hold the appointment and the balance when
we're finished. I can take that over the phone or send you a secure payment link."

"Any areas you want us to focus on, or products we should avoid?"
```

---

## Script 5: Move-Out Clean

**Goal:** Qualify scope, higher deposit.

```
"Move-out cleans are one of our specialties. Is the home completely empty, or
are there still belongings inside?"

"How many bedrooms, bathrooms, and approximate square footage?"

"Do you need inside the oven, refrigerator, all cabinets, and garage swept?"

"Based on that, you're looking at $[Price range]. I'll send a detailed quote
to your email. We require 50% deposit to schedule move-outs."

"When is your walk-through or key handoff deadline?"
```

---

## Script 6: Complaint Call — Missed Areas

**Goal:** De-escalate, fix, retain.

```
"I'm really sorry to hear that — that's not the standard we hold ourselves to.
Thank you for telling us directly."

"Can you tell me specifically which areas weren't up to par?"

[Listen without interrupting]

"I'd like to send [cleaner name / our lead] back to re-clean those areas at no
charge. We have [Day] at [Time] — does that work?"

"We'll also note this in your file so your cleaner knows exactly what to focus
on next visit. Your satisfaction matters to us."
```

---

## Script 7: Complaint Call — Damage

**Goal:** Document, empathize, escalate properly.

```
"I'm so sorry that happened. Let's figure this out together."

"Can you describe what was damaged and when you noticed it?"

"I'm going to pull our documentation from that visit and have our owner/manager
call you back within [timeframe]."

"In the meantime, could you send photos to [email/text]? That helps us resolve
this quickly."

[Do NOT admit fault or promise payment on the spot — investigate first]
```

---

## Script 8: Cancellation Request

**Goal:** Enforce policy, offer reschedule.

```
"I can help with that. Your appointment is scheduled for [Day/Time]."

"Our policy requires 48 hours notice for cancellations without a fee. Based on
today's date, [there is no fee / there would be a $X fee]."

"Would you prefer to reschedule instead? I have [options]."

[If recurring cancellation]
"We're sorry to see you go. Per our agreement, recurring service requires
30 days notice. Your last scheduled visit would be [Date]."
```

---

## Script 9: Returning Missed Call (Voicemail Callback)

**Goal:** Book within 5 minutes of callback.

```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company] — I'm returning your call from
earlier about cleaning service."

"I have your number as [confirm]. Is now a good time for a quick quote?"

[If voicemail again]
"Hi [Name], [Company] returning your call. I can text you a link to book
online or call us back at [number]. We have openings this week. Thanks!"
```

---

## Script 10: Commercial / Office Inquiry

**Goal:** Qualify, schedule walkthrough.

```
"Thanks for calling. Commercial cleaning is a bit different from residential —
I'd like to understand your space first."

"How large is the office in square footage, and how many restrooms?"

"How often do you need service — nightly, weekly, or custom?"

"We typically do a walkthrough before quoting commercial work. Can I schedule
[Your Name] to visit [Day] at [Time]?"
```

---

## Script 11: Airbnb / STR Host

**Goal:** Fast turnaround, volume pricing.

```
"We work with several Airbnb hosts in [City]. Turnover includes clean, linens,
restock, and photo-ready staging."

"How many bedrooms, and what's your average turnover time window?"

"Our turnover rate starts at $[Price]. For multiple properties or 8+ turnovers
per month, we offer volume pricing."

"Do you use a lockbox or smart lock? We can coordinate codes through your PMS."
```

---

## Script 12: Out of Service Area

**Goal:** Decline gracefully, protect margins.

```
"Thanks for reaching out! Unfortunately [City] is outside our current service
area — we focus on [list cities] to make sure we arrive on time every visit."

"I'd recommend searching Yelp for house cleaners in [their city]. If we expand
that direction, we'll post on our website. Best of luck!"
```

## Phone Etiquette Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Answer within 3 rings | Every missed call is a competitor's gain |
| Smile while talking | It changes your tone |
| Use client's name twice | Builds rapport |
| Never quote without qualifying | Bedroom count matters |
| Always offer two time slots | Assumptive close |
| Text confirmation before hanging up | Reduces no-shows |
