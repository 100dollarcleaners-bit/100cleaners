## Purpose

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|

| **Business Hours** | Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (adjust for your hours) |

## Cancellation Policy

### Standard Residential Cleaning

| Notice Given | Cancellation Fee |
|--------------|------------------|
| **48 hours or more** before appointment | No fee. Deposit refunded in full if applicable. |
| **24 to 48 hours** before appointment | **50%** of the scheduled service price |
| **Less than 24 hours** before appointment | **100%** of the scheduled service price |
| **No-show** (we arrive and cannot access home) | **100%** of the scheduled service price |

*Example: A $150 regular clean cancelled 30 hours before the appointment incurs a $75 fee.*

### Deep Clean, Move-Out & Premium Services

| Notice Given | Cancellation Fee |
|--------------|------------------|
| **72 hours or more** | No fee. Deposit refunded. |
| **48 to 72 hours** | **50%** of quoted price |
| **Less than 48 hours** | **100%** of quoted price (deposit forfeited) |
| **No-show** | **100%** of quoted price |

### Recurring Service Cancellation

- Clients on recurring schedules may cancel future services with **30 days written notice** (email or text to [your email]).
- Individual visit cancellations within a recurring plan are subject to the standard notice periods above.
- We reserve the right to discontinue recurring service with 30 days notice for repeated late cancellations or payment issues.

## Rescheduling Policy

| Notice Given | Fee |
|--------------|-----|
| **48+ hours** | Free reschedule, one time per month |
| **24–48 hours** | $25 rescheduling fee OR treated as cancellation (50%) — your choice per company policy |
| **Less than 24 hours** | Treated as cancellation (100% fee); new appointment requires new booking and deposit |

**To reschedule:** Reply to your confirmation text, email [your email], or call [your phone] during business hours.

---

## Deposit Policy

- Deposits for deep cleans and move-outs are **non-refundable** if cancelled within the fee window stated above.
- Deposits are **applied to your final invoice** when service is completed.
- Deposits are **fully refundable** if you cancel outside the fee window.

---

## How Fees Are Charged

- Cancellation fees are charged to the **card on file** provided at booking.
- You will receive email confirmation of any charge within 24 hours.
- Disputes must be submitted in writing within 7 days of the charge.

---

## Exceptions (At Our Discretion)

We may waive cancellation fees for:

- Documented emergencies (hospitalization, death in family)
- Severe weather or natural disasters
- First-time occurrence for established recurring clients (one grace per year)

**We do not waive fees for:** forgotten appointments, duplicate bookings, or "something came up" without emergency documentation.

---

## Access Issues & Lockouts

If our team arrives during the scheduled window and cannot enter the home (no key, wrong code, no answer):

- We will wait **15 minutes** and attempt to contact you.
- If access is not provided, this is treated as a **no-show** and charged at **100%**.
- We will attempt to reschedule at our availability; a new deposit may be required.
