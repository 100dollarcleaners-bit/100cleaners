## Professionalism & Conduct

- [ ] Cleaner in proper uniform and presentable appearance
- [ ] Arrived on time and followed check-in protocol
- [ ] Professional communication with client (if present)
- [ ] Phone use limited to work-related tasks only
- [ ] Respectful of client property — no unauthorized moving or opening
- [ ] Shoe covers or indoor shoes in use per policy

## Process & Technique

- [ ] Correct products used for each surface (no damage risk)
- [ ] Top-to-bottom, back-to-front cleaning sequence followed
- [ ] Color-coded cloth system used correctly (if applicable)
- [ ] Dwell time allowed on disinfectants and degreasers
- [ ] No cross-contamination between bathroom and kitchen cloths
- [ ] Vacuum lines visible on carpet; hard floors streak-free

## Kitchen — Spot Check

- [ ] Countertops free of crumbs, grease, and water rings
- [ ] Stovetop clean; burners and drip pans addressed
- [ ] Sink scrubbed and polished
- [ ] Appliance exteriors wiped
- [ ] Floor edges and under toe-kicks checked

## Bathrooms — Spot Check

- [ ] Toilet fully sanitized (including base and behind)
- [ ] Shower/tub free of soap scum and mildew
- [ ] Mirrors streak-free; fixtures polished
- [ ] Grout and caulk lines addressed per service level
- [ ] Floor corners and baseboards clean

## Bedrooms & Living — Spot Check

- [ ] Dust-free on surfaces, shelves, and décor
- [ ] Beds made to standard (if in scope)
- [ ] Upholstery vacuumed where applicable
- [ ] Light fixtures and ceiling fans dusted (if in scope)
- [ ] Trash removed; liners replaced

## Safety & Compliance

- [ ] Chemicals labeled and stored safely during job
- [ ] No mixing of incompatible products
- [ ] Ladder or step stool used safely (if applicable)
- [ ] Wet floor areas managed; no slip hazard left
- [ ] Client pets and children safety protocols followed

## Documentation

- [ ] Before/after photos taken per SOP
- [ ] Job clocked in/out accurately
- [ ] Notes completed in CRM
- [ ] Any client complaints or concerns documented
