## Kitchen — Deep

- [ ] Degrease stovetop, drip pans, and hood exterior
- [ ] Clean inside microwave (walls, ceiling, turntable)
- [ ] Wipe top of refrigerator and tall appliances
- [ ] Clean inside sink drain area and faucet aerator buildup
- [ ] Wipe inside of trash can
- [ ] Clean baseboards along kitchen perimeter
- [ ] Wipe switch plates and outlet covers
- [ ] Detail clean around small appliances (toaster, coffee maker area)

## Bathrooms — Deep

- [ ] Scrub grout lines and tile surfaces
- [ ] Remove soap scum and hard water buildup on glass and fixtures
- [ ] Clean inside vanity drawers (empty first if in scope)
- [ ] Wipe inside of medicine cabinet (if in scope)
- [ ] Scrub shower door tracks and tub caulking line
- [ ] Polish chrome and metal fixtures to shine
- [ ] Clean baseboards and vent covers
- [ ] Wipe doors (both sides) and door frames

## Bedrooms — Deep

- [ ] Dust ceiling fan blades and light fixtures
- [ ] Dust baseboards throughout
- [ ] Wipe window sills, tracks, and ledges
- [ ] Vacuum under bed (if accessible)
- [ ] Dust closet shelves and rods (exterior / accessible areas)
- [ ] Wipe door frames and switch plates
- [ ] Clean mirrors and glass surfaces

## Living & Dining — Deep

- [ ] Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures
- [ ] Dust baseboards throughout
- [ ] Wipe window sills, tracks, and ledges
- [ ] Dust blinds or wipe shutters (both sides if accessible)
- [ ] Vacuum under couch and major furniture (if movable)
- [ ] Wipe doors, door frames, and switch plates
- [ ] Clean glass on coffee table or display cabinets

## Floors — Deep

- [ ] Vacuum all carpet with slow overlapping passes
- [ ] Edge vacuum along all baseboards and stairs
- [ ] Mop hard floors with attention to corners and along baseboards
- [ ] Spot-treat carpet stains
- [ ] Clean stair treads and risers (if applicable)

## Additional Areas (If In Scope)

- [ ] Laundry room — wipe appliances, counters, and floor
- [ ] Interior doors wiped on both sides
- [ ] Light switch plates and outlet covers wiped home-wide
- [ ] Cobweb removal in corners and ceiling junctions
- [ ] Air vent covers dusted or wiped (exterior)

## Deep Clean Close-Out

- [ ] All regular clean standards met in every room
- [ ] Extended items completed per quote/scope
- [ ] Before/after photos taken for key transformation areas
- [ ] Client walkthrough offered for deep clean jobs
