## Photo Standards (Every Job)

- [ ] Photos taken in good lighting — flash off when possible
- [ ] Same angle and framing for before AND after shots
- [ ] No people, faces, or personal documents visible in photos
- [ ] Photos uploaded to CRM/app before leaving property
- [ ] File names or tags include client name and date per SOP

## Required — Before Photos (Upon Arrival)

- [ ] Kitchen — countertops and sink area
- [ ] Kitchen — stovetop (if visibly soiled)
- [ ] Primary bathroom — vanity and sink
- [ ] Primary bathroom — toilet and shower/tub (if visibly soiled)
- [ ] Living area — main surfaces and floor
- [ ] Any pre-existing damage or stains (close-up + context shot)
- [ ] Any area client flagged as priority concern

## Required — After Photos (Before Departure)

- [ ] Kitchen — countertops and sink (same angle as before)
- [ ] Kitchen — stovetop
- [ ] Primary bathroom — vanity and sink (same angle as before)
- [ ] Primary bathroom — toilet and shower/tub
- [ ] Living area — main surfaces and floor (same angle as before)
- [ ] Master bedroom — bed made and surfaces (if in scope)

## Deep Clean & Move-Out — Additional Photos

- [ ] Inside oven (before and after)
- [ ] Inside refrigerator (before and after)
- [ ] Inside microwave (before and after)
- [ ] Baseboards in kitchen and bathrooms (after)
- [ ] Inside cabinets or drawers (if cleaned — before and after)
- [ ] Closets emptied and cleaned (move-out)
- [ ] Garage or laundry room (if in scope)

## Marketing-Quality Shots (When Appropriate)

- [ ] Dramatic before/after pair approved for social media use
- [ ] Client consent obtained if faces or identifying details visible
- [ ] Sent to manager for marketing library

## Photo QC — Manager Review

- [ ] Before/after pairs match and tell a clear story
- [ ] Image quality acceptable (not blurry or too dark)
- [ ] No privacy violations (mail, prescriptions, children's items)
- [ ] Filed correctly for client record and dispute protection
