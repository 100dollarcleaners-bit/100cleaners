## General — Whole Property

- [ ] All rooms confirmed empty and accessible
- [ ] Pre-existing damage documented with photos before cleaning
- [ ] All light fixtures dusted and bulbs wiped (if accessible)
- [ ] Ceiling fans cleaned
- [ ] All interior doors, frames, and handles wiped both sides
- [ ] All switch plates and outlet covers wiped
- [ ] Baseboards wiped throughout entire property
- [ ] Air vent covers removed, washed, and reinstalled (if in scope)
- [ ] Cobwebs removed from ceilings and corners
- [ ] All closets empty, shelves wiped, rods and floors cleaned
- [ ] Interior windows and tracks cleaned (if in scope)
- [ ] Blinds dusted or wiped (if left in unit)

## Kitchen — Move-Out

- [ ] All cabinets and drawers cleaned inside and out
- [ ] Countertops and backsplash degreased and sanitized
- [ ] Sink scrubbed, polished, and drain area detailed
- [ ] Stovetop, drip pans, and hood thoroughly degreased
- [ ] Oven cleaned inside (racks, walls, door glass)
- [ ] Refrigerator cleaned inside and out (including coils area if accessible)
- [ ] Dishwasher cleaned inside (filter, door edges, tub)
- [ ] Microwave cleaned inside and out
- [ ] Exhaust fan filter cleaned or replaced (if in scope)
- [ ] Floor swept, mopped, and edges detailed

## Bathrooms — Move-Out (Each)

- [ ] Toilet fully sanitized inside, out, base, and behind
- [ ] Shower/tub descaled; grout and caulk scrubbed
- [ ] Shower doors, tracks, and enclosures detailed
- [ ] Sink, vanity, and cabinet interiors cleaned
- [ ] Mirrors and glass polished streak-free
- [ ] Exhaust fan cover cleaned
- [ ] Floor scrubbed; corners and behind toilet addressed
- [ ] Towel bars, hooks, and fixtures polished

## Bedrooms & Living Areas — Move-Out

- [ ] All walls spot-cleaned (scuffs, marks — no full paint)
- [ ] Closet systems fully wiped — shelves, rods, floors
- [ ] Carpet vacuumed with edge detail OR hard floors mopped
- [ ] Window sills and tracks cleaned
- [ ] Fireplace area swept (if applicable and in scope)

## Laundry & Utility

- [ ] Washer interior wiped; gasket and dispenser cleaned
- [ ] Dryer interior wiped; lint trap cleaned
- [ ] Utility sink scrubbed
- [ ] Floor swept and mopped
- [ ] Water heater and furnace exterior dusted (if accessible)

## Garage & Exterior Entry (If In Scope)

- [ ] Garage floor swept; cobwebs removed
- [ ] Entry door, threshold, and porch swept/wiped
- [ ] Exterior light fixtures wiped (reachable without ladder over 6 ft)

## Final Move-Out Inspection

- [ ] Every room matches move-out standard — landlord/realtor ready
- [ ] All trash and cleaning debris removed from property
- [ ] No personal items or supplies left behind
- [ ] All faucets off; no running water or leaks noted
- [ ] Lights off; thermostat set per client/landlord instructions
- [ ] Doors locked; keys returned per client instructions
- [ ] Complete before/after photo set uploaded
- [ ] Move-out completion report sent to client and/or property manager
