## Final Room-by-Room Walkthrough

- [ ] Every in-scope room re-walked with fresh eyes
- [ ] No cleaning supplies, cloths, or trash bags left behind
- [ ] All trash taken to exterior bins (if in scope)
- [ ] Wet floors dry or "wet floor" area clearly avoided
- [ ] Windows and blinds left as found (unless in scope to clean)

## Kitchen & Bathrooms — Double Check

- [ ] Sinks empty, polished, and dry
- [ ] Toilets flushed; lids down
- [ ] Towels and bath mats straightened (not replaced unless instructed)
- [ ] Counter items returned to original positions
- [ ] No chemical smell overpowering — area ventilated if needed

## Security & Home Care

- [ ] All interior doors left as client requested (open/closed)
- [ ] Lights turned off or left as found
- [ ] Thermostat unchanged unless client asked otherwise
- [ ] Windows locked if opened for ventilation
- [ ] Exterior doors locked; garage door closed per instructions
- [ ] Alarm set if client provided code and instructions

## Client Handoff

- [ ] Client greeted for brief walkthrough (if home and available)
- [ ] Highlighted any areas that needed extra attention or could not be completed
- [ ] Reported any damage found during clean (with photos if applicable)
- [ ] Confirmed next appointment or reminded client how to rebook
- [ ] Thanked client; left business card or review request per SOP

## Documentation & Close-Out

- [ ] After photos taken per company SOP (see Photo Checklist)
- [ ] Job notes completed in CRM (time, issues, supplies used)
- [ ] Clock out / job marked complete in app
- [ ] Review request sent (text or email) if not done in person
- [ ] Manager notified immediately of any complaints, damage, or re-clean needs

## Vehicle & Departure

- [ ] Dirty linens and trash removed from home
- [ ] Supplies repacked; vehicle ready for next job
- [ ] Left property professionally — no litter in driveway or yard
