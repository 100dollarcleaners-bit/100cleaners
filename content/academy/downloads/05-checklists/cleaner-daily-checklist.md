## Personal Readiness

- [ ] Uniform clean, pressed, and professional (branded polo or all-black)
- [ ] Closed-toe non-slip shoes in good condition
- [ ] Hair secured; minimal jewelry; nails safe for cleaning work
- [ ] Phone charged; scheduling app and GPS accessible
- [ ] Photo ID and company info available if required

## Vehicle & Equipment Check

- [ ] Vehicle clean and organized inside
- [ ] Vacuum charged / cord checked / bags or canister empty
- [ ] Mop, bucket, and mop heads loaded
- [ ] Microfiber cloths stocked (color-coded sets if used)
- [ ] All-purpose cleaner, glass cleaner, bathroom cleaner stocked
- [ ] Disinfectant and degreaser available
- [ ] Scrub brushes, sponges, and magic erasers stocked
- [ ] Trash bags, gloves, and shoe covers packed
- [ ] Extension cord and step stool (if needed for today's jobs)
- [ ] Spare supplies bag for mid-day restock

## Job Prep Review

- [ ] Today's schedule reviewed in app or dispatch sheet
- [ ] Addresses, gate codes, and parking notes confirmed
- [ ] Service type confirmed for each job (regular / deep / move-out)
- [ ] Special client instructions read (pets, products, focus areas)
- [ ] Estimated drive time to first job calculated — departure time set
- [ ] Client confirmation text sent if required by SOP

## Communication & Safety

- [ ] Manager or dispatcher notified of any delays before leaving
- [ ] Emergency contact numbers saved
- [ ] First aid kit in vehicle (if company policy)
- [ ] Weather-appropriate gear packed (umbrella, ice scraper, etc.)

## End of Morning Prep

- [ ] Clock in / start day logged per company policy
- [ ] Departure time recorded: _________
- [ ] Ready to represent 100% Cleaner with excellence
