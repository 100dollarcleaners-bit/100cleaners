## 1. Scope of Services

Company agrees to provide residential cleaning services ("Services") at the Service Address described above, in accordance with the service type, add-ons, and specifications listed in **Exhibit A — Service Details** attached hereto.

Standard service types include:

| Service | Description | Standard Rate |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| **Regular Maintenance Clean** | Dusting, floors, kitchen surfaces, bathrooms, trash removal, general tidy | **$150** |
| **Deep Clean** | All regular services plus baseboards, detail work, buildup removal, extra kitchen/bath attention | **$300** |

Company will furnish all cleaning supplies and equipment unless Client requests use of Client-provided products (noted in Exhibit A).

## 2. Scheduling & Access

**2.1 Appointments.** Services will be performed on the date(s) and time window(s) agreed upon by the Parties and confirmed in writing (email, text, or booking system).

**2.2 Access.** Client agrees to provide safe, timely access to the Service Address via the method described in Exhibit A (key, lockbox, smart lock, etc.). Client is responsible for ensuring access devices work on the day of service.

**2.3 Delays.** If Client fails to provide access within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time, Company may reschedule the appointment and charge a lockout fee of $_______ or retain the deposit, at Company's discretion.

**2.4 Recurring Services.** For recurring clients, services continue on the agreed frequency (weekly, biweekly, monthly) until modified or terminated per Section 8.

**3.1 Service Fees.** Client agrees to pay the fees listed in Exhibit A. Standard rates: Regular Clean **$150** | Deep Clean **$300**.

**3.3 Balance Due.** The remaining balance is due upon completion of service unless otherwise agreed in writing. Accepted payment methods: _______________.

**3.5 Price Changes.** Company may adjust pricing with _______________ days written notice to Client. Client may cancel recurring service if new pricing is not acceptable.

**3.6 Tips.** Gratuities are appreciated but never required. Tips go directly to the cleaning professional(s) who performed the service.

## 4. Cancellations & Rescheduling

**4.1 Client Cancellation.** Client must provide at least _______________ hours notice to cancel or reschedule without penalty. Cancellations with less notice may result in forfeiture of the deposit or a cancellation fee of $_______.

**4.2 Company Cancellation.** Company reserves the right to cancel or reschedule due to weather, illness, equipment failure, or emergencies. Client will receive prompt notice and priority rescheduling.

**4.3 Satisfaction Guarantee.** If Client is not satisfied with the quality of service, Client must notify Company within **24 hours** of service completion. Company will re-clean the identified areas at no additional charge. This guarantee does not apply to areas not included in the scope of work or conditions requiring services beyond standard residential cleaning.

---

## 5. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

- Provide accurate home information, access instructions, and pet details
- Secure valuables, cash, jewelry, firearms, medications, and sensitive documents
- Ensure pets are secured if they may pose a safety risk
- Disclose any hazardous conditions (mold, pest infestation, biohazards, structural damage)
- Maintain a reasonably safe work environment (adequate lighting, stable flooring, working utilities)
- Notify Company of any allergies or product sensitivities
- Inform Company of any changes to access codes, alarm systems, or service preferences

---

## 6. Limitation of Services

Services do **not** include:

- Biohazard, hoarding, or crime scene cleanup
- Mold remediation or asbestos abatement
- Exterior window washing (unless add-on selected)
- Moving heavy furniture or appliances
- Childcare, pet care, or personal errands (unless add-on selected)
- Repairs of any kind
- Cleaning of areas designated off-limits by Client

Company reserves the right to refuse or stop service if conditions are unsafe, outside scope, or misrepresented by Client.

---

## 7. Damage, Breakage & Liability

**7.1 Standard of Care.** Company and its personnel will perform Services with reasonable care consistent with industry standards for residential cleaning.

**7.2 Reporting.** Client must report any alleged damage within **24 hours** of service completion. Company is not liable for claims reported after this period.

**7.3 Accidental Damage.** If Company personnel accidentally damage Client property due to negligence during service, Company's liability is limited to the **lesser of**: (a) the actual cost of repair or replacement, or (b) **$_____________** per occurrence, subject to Section 7.5.

**7.4 Exclusions.** Company is **not liable** for:

- Pre-existing damage, wear, or defective items
- Damage to improperly installed fixtures, loose hardware, or fragile items not secured by Client
- Staining, discoloration, or warping caused by applying appropriate cleaning products to surfaces not suitable for those products (when Client has not disclosed material restrictions)
- Damage to items valued over $_____________ unless listed in Exhibit B — Items of Special Value
- Consequential, indirect, or incidental damages
- Loss or theft of unsecured valuables, cash, or jewelry

**7.5 Insurance.** Company maintains general liability insurance. A certificate of insurance is available upon request. Client's sole remedy for covered claims is through Company's insurance process or the liability cap in Section 7.3.

**7.6 Client-Provided Products.** If Client requests use of Client-supplied cleaning products, Company is not responsible for adverse reactions, surface damage, or health effects resulting from those products.

## 8. Term & Termination

**8.1 One-Time Services.** This Agreement applies to the single service date listed in Exhibit A unless converted to recurring service.

**8.2 Recurring Services.** Recurring service continues until either Party provides _______________ days written notice of termination.

**8.3 Immediate Termination.** Company may terminate this Agreement immediately if Client engages in harassment, threats, unsafe conditions, non-payment, or material breach of this Agreement.

## 9. Independent Contractor Status

Company's cleaning personnel are employees or independent contractors of Company, not Client. Client will not direct hiring, firing, or daily supervision of individual cleaners. All service requests and concerns should be directed to Company management.

---

## 10. Privacy & Confidentiality

Company respects Client privacy. Personnel will not disclose Client personal information, home details, or access credentials to third parties except as required by law or to perform Services. Company may use before/after photos of work areas (without personal items visible) for quality control and marketing only with Client's written consent.

## 11. Indemnification

**11.1 Client Indemnification.** Client agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Company, its owners, employees, and contractors from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from: (a) Client's breach of this Agreement; (b) undisclosed hazardous conditions at the Service Address; (c) Client-provided products or instructions; or (d) Client's failure to secure pets or valuables.

**11.2 Company Indemnification.** Company agrees to indemnify Client from claims arising solely from Company's gross negligence or willful misconduct, subject to the liability limits in Section 7.
