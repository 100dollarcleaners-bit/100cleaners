# Storage & Organization Guide

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

Chaos in your storage unit becomes chaos in client homes. This guide covers how to organize supplies, restock efficiently, and keep vehicles job-ready.

---

## Why Storage Systems Matter

| Problem | Cost |
|---------|------|
| Cleaner arrives without right products | Wasted drive time, poor quality |
| Duplicate buying | Eats 5–10% of supply budget |
| Expired or leaked chemicals | Safety hazard, wasted money |
| No client-specific items | Forgotten preferences, complaints |

A organized back-end makes your front-end look professional.

## Storage Unit Layout

### Choosing a Unit

| Team Size | Recommended Size | Monthly Cost (varies) |
|-----------|------------------|----------------------|
| Solo + 1–2 cleaners | 5×10 or 10×10 | $75–$150 |
| 3–5 cleaners | 10×15 or 10×20 | $150–$300 |
| 6+ cleaners | 10×20+ or warehouse | $300+ |

**Location:** Within 15 minutes of your densest service area. Drive time to storage kills margins.

### Zone Labels

Use colored tape or labels on floor/shelves:

| Zone | Color | Contents |
|------|-------|----------|
| A — Chemicals | Red | Sprays, degreasers, disinfectants |
| B — Consumables | Blue | Paper towels, trash bags, sponges |
| C — Client bins | Green | Per-client dedicated items |
| D — Equipment | Yellow | Vacuums, mop heads, tools |
| E — Admin | White | Forms, spare keys, marketing |

## One Bin Per Client System

For recurring clients with specific preferences, assign a dedicated bin.

### What Goes in a Client Bin

| Item | Example |
|------|---------|
| Preferred products | Client-provided or your approved brand |
| Special instructions card | Laminated, taped inside lid |
| Spare supplies | Extra microfiber in their preferred color |
| Key tag | Labeled, on hook above bin |
| Add-on kit | Pet-safe spray if they have dogs |

### Bin Label Template

```
CLIENT: [Last Name]
ADDRESS: [Street — city only for privacy in unit]
CLEANER: [Primary assigned]
PRODUCTS: [Mrs. Meyer's / hypoallergenic / etc.]
NOTES: No bleach. Use side gate. Dog friendly.
```

### When to Use Client Bins

- **Yes:** Weekly/bi-weekly recurring, 6+ month clients
- **Yes:** Allergy-sensitive or product-specific requests
- **No:** One-time deep cleans (use general stock)
- **No:** Move-outs (disposable mindset)

## Shelving & Container Standards

### Recommended Containers

| Item | Type | Qty |
|------|------|-----|
| Client bins | 16–27 qt clear plastic with lid | 1 per recurring client |
| Chemical storage | Original bottles in locked cabinet | — |
| Microfiber | Color-coded bins (blue bath, yellow kitchen) | 3–4 |
| Caddies | Portable for vehicle | 1 per active cleaner |
| Bulk items | Stackable bins with lids | As needed |

### Chemical Safety

- Store upright, lids tight, away from heat
- SDS sheets in admin zone (or digital folder)
- Never mix products in storage
- Separate acids from bases
- Locked cabinet if unit is shared-access facility

## Restocking System

### Who Restocks

| Model | Who | When |
|-------|-----|------|
| Owner-managed | You | Weekly |
| Cleaner self-serve | Each cleaner | After last job Friday |
| Lead cleaner | Lead | Sunday prep for week |

### Weekly Restock Workflow

1. **Inventory check** — use [Weekly Inventory Sheet](weekly-inventory-sheet.csv)
2. **Pull low items** from bulk shelving to caddies
3. **Refill client bins** — check instruction cards
4. **Laundry** — microfiber and mop heads (never fabric softener)
5. **Trash** — empty chemical bottles per local disposal rules
6. **Log order** — place supply order by cutoff day

### Par Level System

Set minimum quantities — reorder when you hit "par."

| Product | Par Level (per cleaner caddy) |
|---------|------------------------------|
| All-purpose cleaner | 1 full bottle |
| Bathroom cleaner | 1 full bottle |
| Glass cleaner | 1 full bottle |
| Microfiber cloths | 12 clean |
| Trash bags (kitchen size) | 1 roll |
| Sponges | 2 |
| Gloves | 5 pairs |

## Vehicle Organization

### Cleaner Vehicle Setup (If They Use Own Car)

Provide a **company caddy** that moves car to car — standardize what's inside.

### Caddy Contents Checklist

- [ ] All-purpose cleaner
- [ ] Bathroom cleaner
- [ ] Glass cleaner
- [ ] 12 microfiber cloths (color-coded)
- [ ] Scrub brushes (toilet, grout)
- [ ] Extendable duster
- [ ] Gloves
- [ ] Trash bags
- [ ] Small vacuum (if not stored in vehicle permanently)
- [ ] Client keys (day of job only — return same day)

### Vehicle Rules

1. Chemicals upright in trunk, not rolling around
2. Vacuum secured — not a projectile
3. No food trash mixed with supplies
4. Client keys never overnight in vehicle unless locked safe
5. Weekly vehicle wipe-down (professional appearance)

### Company Van/Truck (When You Scale)

| Zone | Contents |
|------|----------|
| Driver side | Personal, phone mount, GPS |
| Passenger floor | Day's caddies (1 per job) |
| Rear left | Vacuums, mop bucket, large tools |
| Rear right | Bulk restock, paper goods |
| Locked box | Keys, backup supplies |

## Key Management

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Check-out | Sign key in/out log when leaving storage |
| Same-day return | Keys return to storage or manager by end of day |
| No duplicates | Without client written consent |
| Lost key | Client notified immediately, rekey cost policy applies |
| Digital | Lockbox codes in app only — not group chat |

## Laundry Protocol

Microfiber and mop heads should be washed **at storage or commercial laundry** — not at client homes.

### Wash Rules

- Separate by color
- No fabric softener (ruins absorbency)
- Low heat or air dry
- Replace when stained or frayed (budget monthly)
