# Local Marketing Setup Guide

**The Cleaning CEO Blueprint — 100% Cleaner Academy**

## Your Brand Foundation

### Company Name Tips
- Easy to spell and say on the phone
- Avoid limiting geography in the name unless you're staying local forever
- Check domain, Instagram, and Google availability before committing

### Logo & Colors
- Keep it clean and professional — navy, white, gold, or blue/green work well
- Use the same logo everywhere (uniform, vehicle, website, invoices)
- Export PNG (transparent) and SVG versions

### Website Must-Haves
- Services and pricing (or "starting at" ranges)
- Book online button or contact form
- Before/after gallery
- Reviews/testimonials
- Service area (cities/neighborhoods)
- Phone number prominently displayed
- Mobile-friendly design

---

## Google Business Profile

1. Go to business.google.com and claim/create your listing
2. Use exact business name (match LLC or DBA)
3. Select category: **House Cleaning Service**
4. Add service area (don't show home address unless required)
5. Upload 10+ photos: team, supplies, before/after, logo
6. Write description with keywords: "residential cleaning," "deep clean," your city
7. Add services with descriptions
8. Enable messaging
9. Post weekly updates (tips, before/after, promotions)
10. Ask every happy client for a review — send direct link

**Ranking tips:** Complete every field, respond to all reviews within 24 hours, post photos weekly, keep NAP (name, address, phone) consistent everywhere online.

---

## Yelp

1. Claim your business at biz.yelp.com
2. Upload 15+ high-quality photos
3. Fill out all specialties and service offerings
4. Respond to every review — good and bad
5. Do NOT buy leads until you have 10+ reviews and can convert inquiries
6. See Yelp Masterclass Guide for full strategy

## Social Media

### Instagram
- Bio: City + service + book link
- Post 3–5x/week: before/after reels, tips, team highlights
- Use local hashtags: #LACleaner #LosAngelesCleaning #[YourCity]Cleaning
- Stories: day-in-the-life, polls, Q&A

### Facebook
- Create Business Page (not personal profile)
- Join local community groups (follow group rules — no spam)
- Run $5–10/day local awareness ads once you have 5+ reviews
- Enable Facebook Messenger for inquiries

### Nextdoor
- Register as a local business
- Offer neighbor discount for first 10 clients
- Respond quickly to recommendation requests
- One post per week max — be helpful, not salesy

---

## Getting Your First Reviews

1. **Ask in person** at the end of a great clean: "Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review?"
2. **Text within 2 hours** with direct review link
3. **Email follow-up** next day with links to Google and Yelp
4. **Make it easy** — save direct review URLs in your phone notes
5. **Never incentivize reviews** with discounts (against Google/Yelp policies)
6. **Never buy fake reviews** — platforms will penalize or ban you

## Referral System (Launch Version)

- Referrer gets **$25 off** next clean
- New client gets **$25 off** first clean
- Track in spreadsheet: Referrer Name | New Client | Date | Credit Applied
- Mention referrals on invoices and follow-up texts
