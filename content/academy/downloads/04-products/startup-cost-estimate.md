## Break-Even Math

**Example — Solo owner-operator:**
- Average profit per job: $50 (after supplies, if paying yourself separately)
- Jobs needed to cover $500/mo fixed costs: **10 jobs/month**
- At 4 jobs/week: break-even in ~2.5 weeks on fixed costs only

**Example — Owner + 1 cleaner:**
- Your profit per job: $50 company margin
- 20 jobs/month = $1,000 company profit
- Covers fixed costs + reinvestment

---

## Funding Options

1. **Bootstrap** — Start with one kit, reinvest first 10 job profits
2. **Business credit card** — Pay in full monthly; build credit responsibly
3. **Small business loan** — Only after proven revenue (3+ months books)
4. **Personal savings** — Keep 3 months operating expenses in reserve

> Never borrow more than you can repay from projected month-2 revenue.
